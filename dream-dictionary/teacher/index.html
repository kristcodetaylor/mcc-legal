Teacher in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A teacher stands for learning and being judged. Dream books read the figure as a lesson life is setting, or as your own inner critic; a kind teacher points to guidance and growth. A strict or angry teacher is linked to fear of failure or of not meeting expectations. Being a teacher yourself stands for sharing knowledge and taking responsibility. Meeting an old teacher is read as remembering a lesson from the past that applies again. It can help to notice which part of your life currently feels like a test.
