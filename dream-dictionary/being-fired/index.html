Being fired in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Being fired in a dream is read as insecurity, fear of failure or a feeling of being let go. Tradition sees it as anxiety about your place, rarely as a literal event. Being dismissed in front of colleagues is linked to fear of public judgement, while feeling relieved at losing the job points to a wish to leave a role that no longer fits. Being fired for something you did not do is read as feeling misunderstood. Firing someone else points to cutting ties with a habit or person. It can help to notice where you feel your value is being questioned right now.
