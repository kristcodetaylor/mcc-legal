Kiss in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A kiss stands for affection, acceptance and the wish for closeness. With an unexpected person it is traditionally read as drawing a quality of theirs toward you rather than as a hidden romance. Kissing a partner is read as the state of that bond — warm and easy, or hesitant. A kiss from an ex is one of the most common variants and is usually taken as unfinished feeling, or a look back at the life you had then. Kissing a stranger points to a new side of yourself you are curious about, while a kiss you try to avoid is read as closeness that feels too fast.
