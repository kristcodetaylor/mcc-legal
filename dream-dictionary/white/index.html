White in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

White is read as purity, a clean start and openness. In some traditions it is also the colour of mourning, so dream books ask whether it felt bright or empty. A white room or landscape points to a blank page and possibility, while white clothing is read as innocence or a new role. A white animal is traditionally taken as something rare and meaningful crossing your path. Snow-white light suggests clarity or peace. An all-white scene that feels cold is read as emotional distance.
