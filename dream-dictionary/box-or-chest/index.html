Box or chest in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A closed box is read as a secret or something stored away — memories, feelings, plans not yet opened. Dream books treat opening it as readiness to look at what was put aside. An empty box is linked to disappointment or to space waiting to be filled, while a heavy chest points to responsibilities or family history you carry. A locked box with no key stands for something you cannot yet reach. Finding a treasure chest is read as discovering your own hidden worth. Packing things into boxes carries the image of closing a chapter.
