Raincoat in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A raincoat stands for being ready for emotional weather. Dream books read it as healthy protection against what others pour onto you — or as keeping every feeling from touching you.

A question for you

Has this come up in your dreams before? What was going on in your life then?
