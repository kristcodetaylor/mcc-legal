Music in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Music is read as harmony and feeling that has no words. A song you know traditionally carries the mood of the time you first heard it. Beautiful music is linked to inner peace and good relations, while noise or a song out of tune points to discord in a group or within yourself. Playing an instrument is read as expressing something creative, and singing alone as letting your voice be heard. Music you cannot find the source of carries the image of a quiet call. It can help to notice which melody stays with you after waking.
