Curse in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A curse in a dream is read as fear, resentment or a feeling that something keeps going wrong. Dream books take it as a pattern you want to break, not as real harm. Being cursed by someone is linked to feeling judged or wished ill, while cursing someone yourself points to anger that needs a safer outlet. Breaking a curse is read as freeing yourself from guilt or an old story. A family curse is read as repeated habits across generations. It can help to notice which recurring trouble you have started to see as fate rather than as something you can change.
