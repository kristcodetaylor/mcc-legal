Eyes falling out in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

This unsettling image is read symbolically as a fear of losing perspective or of seeing something you are not ready for. Dream books link it to a moment when your usual way of looking at things no longer works. Holding your own eyes in your hands is sometimes read as being made to look at yourself from the outside. Someone else losing their eyes points to doubts about their judgement or honesty. The dream reflects anxiety about understanding, not a sign about your health.
