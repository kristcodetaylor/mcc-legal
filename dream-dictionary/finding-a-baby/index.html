Finding a baby in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Finding a baby is read as discovering a new beginning or a hidden potential. Tradition also sees in it responsibility for something fragile that has come into your hands.

A question for you

Has this come up in your dreams before? What was going on in your life then?
