River in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A river is read as the course of a life and the passing of time. Crossing it is traditionally a transition; being carried by it, a matter of letting go. A clear, gently flowing river points to things moving at a good pace. A flooding or muddy river is linked to emotions or events that feel out of control. Standing on the bank and watching is read as observing your own life rather than living it. A dry riverbed stands for a sense of stagnation or tiredness; it can help to notice whether you were swimming with the current or against it.
