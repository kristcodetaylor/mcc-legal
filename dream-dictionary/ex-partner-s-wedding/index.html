Ex-partner’s wedding in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Attending an ex’s wedding is read as a chapter being officially closed. Dream books see it as acceptance working its way through, even if the dream itself feels bittersweet. Watching calmly from the crowd is read as genuine release; feeling upset or trying to stop the wedding as a part of you that is not quite ready to let go. Being the one who marries your ex is taken as idealising the past rather than wanting it back. Not being invited is read as feeling left out of a story that once included you. The dream usually describes your own process of moving on, and the feelings it brings are worth noticing gently.
