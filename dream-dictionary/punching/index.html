Punching in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A punch is read as anger or frustration looking for a target. Tradition takes it as a symbol of conflict and of energy held back too long, not as a call to act it out. Punches that land softly or not at all are among the most common versions, read as feeling powerless to make yourself heard. Being punched is linked to feeling attacked or criticised, while punching a wall points to frustration with a situation rather than a person. It can help to ask where you need to set a boundary or say something clearly.
