Overdose in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

An overdose in a dream is read symbolically as “too much” — of pressure, of escape or of something meant to bring relief. Dream books see it as a warning image about overload, a sign that limits have been pushed past what feels safe. Seeing someone else in that state points to deep worry for them. Being saved in the dream is read as a wish for help to arrive. If thoughts of harming yourself are with you when awake, talk to someone you trust or call a local helpline.
