Army in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

An army is read as collective force and organised strength — resources gathered for a struggle, or pressure from a group too large to resist alone. Being part of an army is read as belonging to a team or cause, and sometimes as losing your individual voice. An army marching towards you is taken as feeling overwhelmed by many demands at once. Leading an army is read as confidence and the will to take charge. Being drafted is read as being pulled into a conflict or duty you did not choose. A victorious army is read as success through cooperation. The dream may ask whether you face things alone or with support.
