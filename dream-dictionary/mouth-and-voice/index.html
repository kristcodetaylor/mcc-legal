Mouth and voice in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

The mouth is read as expression — what you say and what you hold in. Being unable to speak traditionally stands for something that wants to be said, or for a fear of not being believed. A sewn or covered mouth is linked to silence imposed by others, while shouting without sound is one of the most common dreams of feeling unheard. Speaking with ease points to confidence and clarity. Food in the mouth that cannot be swallowed is read as words or situations you find hard to accept. The dream may point to a conversation still waiting to happen.
