Teeth in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Teeth falling out is among the most reported dreams anywhere. It is traditionally read as worry about loss, ageing or how you appear to others — and about words you wish you had held back. Crumbling or broken teeth point to feeling powerless or worn down. Pulling out your own tooth is linked to ending something painful or removing a burden; loose teeth are read as insecurity about a decision. Folk tradition in some places tied the dream to family news, while modern readings keep it to stress and self-image. Dream books ask where you feel you are losing your grip.
