Stars in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Stars are read as hope, guidance and far-off wishes. A sky full of stars is traditionally a sign of possibility; a falling star of a wish or a brief chance. Following a bright star points to a goal that gives your life direction. Stars disappearing from the sky are linked to feeling lost or losing faith in a plan. Reaching out to touch a star is read as ambition and longing for something just beyond reach. Stars falling to the earth carry an old image of dreams coming down to everyday reality; dream books ask what wish you are quietly steering by.
