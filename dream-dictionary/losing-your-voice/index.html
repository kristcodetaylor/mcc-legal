Losing your voice in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Opening your mouth and finding no sound is one of the most common dream frustrations. It is read as feeling unheard, or as something important you have not yet found the courage to say. Trying to call for help without a voice points to helplessness in a tense situation. Losing your voice while singing or speaking in public is linked to fear of judgement. Finding it again is read as confidence returning, and it can help to notice whose attention you most want.
