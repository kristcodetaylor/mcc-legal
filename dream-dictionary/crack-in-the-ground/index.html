Crack in the ground in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

When the ground opens in a crack, dream books read it as a split in something that felt solid — a relationship, a plan, a belief. It also stands for what was buried coming up to the light.

A question for you

Has this come up in your dreams before? What was going on in your life then?
