Boat in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A boat carries you across feeling. Tradition reads a steady boat as coping well with emotion, and a leaking or drifting one as feeling at its mercy. Rowing yourself is taken as effort and self-reliance, while being carried by sail or current points to trusting the flow of events. A capsizing boat is read as being overwhelmed for a moment, not as a forecast. A small boat on a vast sea suggests feeling small next to a big change, and reaching the shore is read as arriving somewhere solid again.
