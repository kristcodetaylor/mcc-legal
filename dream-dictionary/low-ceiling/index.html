Low ceiling in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Having to stoop under a low ceiling is read as feeling held back or made smaller than you are. It stands for a situation that leaves you no room to stand up straight.

A question for you

Has this come up in your dreams before? What was going on in your life then?
