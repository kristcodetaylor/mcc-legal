War in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

War is read as conflict that has grown past one person — in a family, a workplace or within yourself. It traditionally stands for tension that needs settling, not as a forecast of real events. Being a soldier points to feeling forced to fight for something, while hiding or fleeing is read as wanting out of a struggle that is not yours. Watching war from a distance suggests worry about the world or about people close to you. Peace arriving in the dream is taken as reconciliation within reach. It can help to ask which of your battles feels the most tiring.
