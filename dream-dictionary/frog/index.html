Frog in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

The frog lives in two worlds and changes form, so tradition reads it as transformation and a leap from one stage of life to the next. In old tales the frog hides a prince, which is why it is linked to hidden worth and to something plain turning out to be precious. A croaking frog is read as a voice that wants to be heard, and many frogs as a season of change or of cleansing rain. A frog jumping away points to an opportunity that is fleeting. Holding one may reflect a readiness to accept what at first seems unappealing.
