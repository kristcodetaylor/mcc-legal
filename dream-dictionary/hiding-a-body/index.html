Hiding a body in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Hiding a body is read as guilt or a secret you are keeping buried. Tradition also takes it as avoiding an issue that does not disappear on its own. Fear of being discovered is linked to worry about something coming to light, while hiding it calmly points to denial. Helping someone else hide it is read as being drawn into another’s secret. The body resurfacing is read as an issue returning until it is faced. It is read symbolically, never as a judgement. It can help to ask what you are trying hardest not to look at.
