Cup or glass in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A cup is read as what you can hold and receive — love, help, experience. A full one traditionally stands for contentment and plenty, a broken one for a feeling that spilled or a hope that cracked. An empty cup is linked to a need for refreshment or for someone to fill it with care. Drinking from a shared cup points to closeness and trust. Spilling a drink is read as losing something through haste. A golden or precious cup is the old image of a reward or an honour, while a chipped one suggests a familiar thing showing wear.
