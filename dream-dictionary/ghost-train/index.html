Ghost train in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A ghost train is read as fear chosen on purpose — frights you know are staged. It can stand for facing anxieties in a safe way, or for being carried through something you cannot steer.

A question for you

Where in your waking life have you met something like this lately?
