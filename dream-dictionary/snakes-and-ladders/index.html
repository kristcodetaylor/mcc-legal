Snakes and ladders in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Snakes and ladders is read as the feeling of sudden rises and setbacks that have little to do with effort. It often appears when progress in waking life seems to depend on luck.

A question for you

Has this come up in your dreams before? What was going on in your life then?
