Marriage proposal in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A proposal stands for commitment offered or wished for — to a person, a path or a part of yourself. Tradition reads the answer given in the dream as your readiness to bind yourself. Receiving a proposal is read as the wish to be chosen and valued; making one as being ready to commit to something new. Saying yes joyfully is taken as openness to change, while refusing is read as protecting your independence or doubting the timing. A proposal from a stranger or an unexpected person is read as an offer of a new role or opportunity. The dream may ask what you are being asked to commit to, and what you truly want to say.
