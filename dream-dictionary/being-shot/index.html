Being shot in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Being hit from a distance is read as a sudden blow — harsh words, criticism or news that arrives without warning. Tradition takes it as hurt that lands before you can protect yourself. Being shot by someone you know is read as fear of their judgement or a feeling that they have wounded you. Being shot by a stranger is taken as general anxiety or vulnerability. Surviving or waking before anything happens is read as resilience, and feeling no pain as numbness to a recent hurt. This is one of the more common frightening dreams, symbolic and not a forecast, and it can help to notice where you feel under fire.
