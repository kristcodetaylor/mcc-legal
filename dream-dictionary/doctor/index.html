Doctor in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A doctor is read as the wish for healing and for someone who knows what is wrong. Tradition sees the figure as your own ability to mend. Visiting a doctor in a dream points to concern about something in your life that needs attention, not necessarily your body. A doctor bringing bad news is linked to anxiety and fear of the unknown rather than to any real diagnosis. Being a doctor stands for wanting to help or fix others; waiting endlessly for a doctor is read as feeling unheard. Dream books ask what in your days is asking to be healed.
