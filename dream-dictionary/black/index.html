Black in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Black is read as the unknown, the hidden and what has not been looked at yet. Tradition ties it to grief, but also to depth, rest and elegance. A black animal — a cat, a dog, a bird — is traditionally read as an instinct or fear stepping out of the shadows, not as bad luck. Black clothes point to mourning, seriousness or a wish to go unnoticed. A black sky or room suggests a time of not knowing, and a black object glowing within it a hidden strength.
