Adoption in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Adoption is read as choosing to belong and choosing to take in. Tradition sees it as welcoming a new part of life, an idea or a person, and making it fully your own. Adopting a child in a dream is read as readiness to nurture a new project or relationship. Discovering that you were adopted is taken as questions about identity and where you truly belong, not as a literal claim. Being given up for adoption is read as fear of rejection or of not being enough. Adopting an animal is read as care and responsibility offered freely. The dream may ask what you are ready to welcome into your life and claim as family.
