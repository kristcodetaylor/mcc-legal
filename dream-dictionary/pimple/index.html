Pimple in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A pimple is read as something irritating surfacing that you would rather keep hidden. Dream books link it to self-consciousness and small frustrations breaking out. A pimple on the face before an important event points to worry about how others see you. Squeezing it is read as wanting to get rid of a problem quickly, even if it leaves a mark. Many pimples suggest a build-up of small stresses that finally show.
