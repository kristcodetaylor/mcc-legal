Candle in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A candle is read as hope, remembrance and a life’s small steady flame. A candle going out traditionally stands for something ending quietly, while lighting one is linked to faith, intention or a wish made in the dark. Many candles are read as celebration or a gathering of memories. A candle that keeps burning in the wind points to resilience. Burning candles at a grave or altar carry the image of honouring those who are gone. A melting candle can reflect energy being used up, and the dream may ask what is quietly draining you.
