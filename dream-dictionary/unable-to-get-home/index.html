Unable to get home in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Trying and failing to get home is read as a longing for safety and belonging that keeps being blocked. It often appears in times of change, when the old sense of where you belong no longer quite fits. Roads that loop back, buses that never arrive or a street that has changed are read as the feeling that familiar paths no longer lead where they used to. Reaching a home that looks wrong points to a sense of self in transition. Some dream books also read it as missing a person rather than a place. It can help to ask what home means to you right now.
