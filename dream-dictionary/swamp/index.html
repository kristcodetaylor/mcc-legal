Swamp in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A swamp is traditionally read as being bogged down — in a situation, a mood or obligations that make it hard to move. It also stands for murky feelings that have not yet found an outlet. Wading through it points to slow, tiring progress, while being lost in a swamp suggests confusion with no clear way out. Creatures hidden in the water are read as fears below the surface. Reaching firm ground is taken as relief and a fresh start.
