Doll or toy in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A toy is read as childhood and a wish for simpler days. A doll or puppet can also stand for feeling handled by someone else, or for controlling others more than you mean to. A broken doll is linked to a lost innocence or a hurt from long ago, while a doll that comes to life is read as a buried feeling waking up. Finding an old toy points to nostalgia and comfort. A creepy doll is a common anxious dream about something that looks harmless but feels wrong. Playing with toys stands for creativity without pressure.
