Eyes in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Eyes are read as awareness — what you see and what you refuse to. Being watched stands for self-consciousness, blindness for something not yet faced, and clear, bright eyes for insight and honesty. Injured or closed eyes are linked to avoiding a truth or feeling unable to see your way. A third eye or many eyes is read as heightened intuition. Eyes changing colour stand for a shift in how you see someone or something. Old folk tradition feared the evil eye, the envy of others; dream books ask what you have been noticing — or choosing not to notice.
