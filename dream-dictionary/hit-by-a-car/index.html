Hit by a car in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Being hit by a car is read as being blindsided by an event or a person. Dream books link it to feeling knocked off balance by circumstances, and see it as an image of shock rather than a prediction. Stepping out without looking points to haste or distraction, while a car that comes from nowhere is read as a change that arrived too fast to prepare for. Getting up unhurt is read as resilience, and seeing someone else hit as worry for them. If you are the driver, it is linked to fear that your actions hurt another. It can help to notice what has caught you off guard lately.
