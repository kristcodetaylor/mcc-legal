Heart in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

The heart stands for love, courage and what matters most. Tradition reads a dream about it as a question about what you truly feel. A beating heart points to vitality and strong emotion; a broken heart is linked to grief, disappointment or love that ended. Giving your heart to someone is read as trust and vulnerability; a heart in pain stands for emotional stress rather than any physical sign. A heart that stops in a dream is read as a pause in feeling, not a literal event. It can help to notice what in your days your heart is asking for.
