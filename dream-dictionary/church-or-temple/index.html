Church or temple in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A sacred place is read as the search for meaning, stillness and something larger than daily life. Tradition takes it as a turn inward, whatever your own beliefs. An empty church or temple is often read as a quiet need for reflection, while a full one points to belonging and shared values. A ruined or locked sacred building is traditionally taken as doubt, or as a source of comfort that feels out of reach. Praying in the dream is read as a wish for guidance or forgiveness. Dream books treat the scene with respect for every faith and read it as personal, not as a message about religion.
