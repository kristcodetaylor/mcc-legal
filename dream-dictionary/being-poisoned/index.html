Being poisoned in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Poison in a dream is read as something harmful taken in without noticing — a toxic influence, a resentment, an idea that slowly does damage. Dream books ask what has been quietly souring your days.

A question for you

Has this come up in your dreams before? What was going on in your life then?
