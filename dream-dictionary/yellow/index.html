Yellow in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Yellow is read as light, warmth and a clear mind. Folk dream books also give it a second face: caution and jealousy. Bright yellow flowers or sunlight point to optimism and new ideas, while a faded or sickly yellow is read as worry or fading energy. A yellow room or yellow clothing suggests cheerfulness or a wish to be noticed. Yellow signs and lights are taken as a call to slow down. The feeling the colour brings in the dream decides which face it shows.
