Dream that seems to come true in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A dream that seems to show the future is read by tradition as intuition putting together what you already sensed. Dream books treat these dreams as a mirror of present worries and hopes, not as a window onto what lies ahead.

A question for you

Has this come up in your dreams before? What was going on in your life then?
