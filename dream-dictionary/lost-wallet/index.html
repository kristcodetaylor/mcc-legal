Lost wallet in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A lost wallet is read as a shaken sense of identity or security, not as an actual loss to come. Tradition sees it as worry about what you would be without your usual supports. Searching frantically is usually read as anxiety about being unprepared or careless. Finding it again stands for recovering confidence, while a stranger returning it points to trust in others. Discovering it was stolen rather than lost is linked to feeling taken advantage of. Such dreams often come during busy or chaotic periods. It can help to ask where something essential feels like it is slipping out of your hands.
