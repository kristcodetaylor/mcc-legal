Bones in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Bones are what remains, so tradition reads them as the core of a matter and as things from the past. A skeleton is an old truth coming to light, or the bare structure of a situation once everything else is stripped away. A broken bone is linked to a strain on your foundations — a plan, a relationship or your own resilience. Digging up bones is read as revisiting history or family stories, and a dog with a bone as stubborn attachment. Clean white bones point to clarity, the essentials made visible at last.
