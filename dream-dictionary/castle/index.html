Castle in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

The castle is a classic symbol of protection and the fortified self. Tradition reads it as strength and ideals, and its walls as the defences you keep around what you value. Living in a castle points to a wish for security or recognition, while being locked inside suggests isolation. A castle under attack is read as feeling your boundaries challenged. A ruined castle is linked to old ambitions or defences that no longer serve.
