Comforting in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Comforting someone is traditionally read as the caring side of yourself coming forward, and sometimes as the comfort you are quietly hoping to receive. Being comforted points to a need for gentleness that daily life does not meet.

A question for you

Does the traditional reading fit your dream, or did it mean something else to you?
