Chocolate in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Chocolate is the classic image of indulgence and affection. It is read as a reward, a romantic gesture, or comfort reached for in a low moment. Receiving chocolate is usually read as being appreciated or loved. Eating too much is linked to guilt about pleasure, while chocolate melting in your hand points to a sweet moment slipping away. Sharing it stands for warmth and closeness. It can help to notice what kind of comfort you are seeking lately.
