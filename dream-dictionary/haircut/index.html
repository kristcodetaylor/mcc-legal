Haircut in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A haircut is read as change, a fresh start or cutting something out of your life — an old habit, a tie, a version of yourself. Cutting your own hair is usually taken as a deliberate decision to change, while someone else cutting it points to change that others are steering. A bad or uneven haircut suggests worry about a choice already made, or about how others now see you. Having your hair cut very short is read as a wish to be lighter and freer. It can help to notice what you have recently wanted to trim away.
