Computer in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A computer is read as the thinking, organising mind — memory, logic and stored information. A crash or freeze is traditionally linked to overload and the need to reset. Working smoothly at one is usually read as feeling capable and in control. Losing files is linked to fear of losing memories or effort, while a computer being hacked points to feeling invaded or exposed. Searching for something on the screen stands for looking for answers you have not found yet. It can help to ask whether your mind feels clear or overloaded lately.
