Missed bus in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Missing the bus stands for feeling that a small chance has gone by. Dream books read it as worry about timing and about keeping up with everyday demands. Running after a bus that pulls away is linked to effort that always seems a step behind, while watching it leave calmly points to accepting a slower pace. Taking the wrong bus is read as doubt about the direction you are going in. Since buses come and go all day, the dream is also read as a reminder that ordinary chances return. It can help to notice where your schedule leaves you no room to breathe.
