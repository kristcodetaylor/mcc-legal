Fox in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

The fox is the traditional symbol of cunning and quick wit. It is read as cleverness of your own — a sign to think one step ahead — or as someone who is not being straight with you. A fox entering a henhouse is linked to a threat to something you care for, and a fox watching you to the feeling of being sized up. A tame or friendly fox points to finding a smart way through a problem. In folk tales the fox also survives by adapting, so the dream may ask where flexibility would serve you better than force.
