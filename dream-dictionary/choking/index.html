Choking in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Choking is read as being unable to swallow a situation or say what needs saying. Dream books link it to words or feelings caught in the throat. Choking on food points to taking on more than you can manage, while choking on hair or strange objects suggests something unpleasant you cannot get rid of. Helping someone who is choking is read as a wish to help them speak or cope. It can help to ask what you have been holding back.
