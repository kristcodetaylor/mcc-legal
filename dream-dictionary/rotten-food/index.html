Rotten food in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Food gone bad is read as something left too long — a chance, a feeling or a relationship that was not tended in time. Opening a fridge full of spoiled food is linked to neglected matters piling up out of sight. Eating rotten food without noticing points to accepting something that does not serve you, while throwing it away is read as clearing out what has expired. Food that rots while you watch carries the fear of good things fading through delay. It is an image of neglect, not a warning about the body.
