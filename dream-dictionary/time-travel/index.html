Time travel in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Travelling through time is read as revisiting the past or imagining the future to understand the present. Dream books see it as regret, nostalgia or curiosity about another path. Going back to childhood or an old relationship is linked to unfinished feelings or a wish to choose differently. Visiting the future is read as hope or worry about where life is heading, not as a view of what is to come. Being stuck in another time and unable to return points to feeling cut off from your present life. It can help to notice which moment the dream chose, and why it matters now.
