Green in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Green is the colour of growth, healing and hope. Dream tradition reads it as something recovering or coming alive, and at times as envy. Fresh green fields or leaves point to a new beginning and steady progress, while a dull or sickly green is read as something stagnant. Green eyes or clothing on another person may be taken as jealousy or admiration. A green light suggests permission to go ahead. Walking through deep green woods is read as returning to your natural rhythm.
