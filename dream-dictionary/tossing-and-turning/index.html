Tossing and turning in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Tossing and turning stands for a mind that cannot settle. Dream books read it as unresolved thoughts or worries that keep returning when you try to rest.

A question for you

Has this come up in your dreams before? What was going on in your life then?
