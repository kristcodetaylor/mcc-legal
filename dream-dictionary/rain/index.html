Rain in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Rain is read as release and cleansing — tears that finally fall, or relief after a dry spell. In farming tradition it is a sign of growth and blessing. Walking in gentle rain is linked to renewal and to washing off what weighs on you. A heavy downpour points to sadness or tension that has built up and needs an outlet. Watching rain from inside is read as reflection, or as keeping your feelings at a safe distance. Rain with sunshine is an old image of mixed emotions and quiet hope; dream books ask what you might be ready to let go of.
