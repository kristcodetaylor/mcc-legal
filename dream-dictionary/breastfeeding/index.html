Breastfeeding in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Breastfeeding is read as nurturing in its most direct form — giving of yourself so that something new can grow. A calm, easy feeding is taken as a close bond and the joy of caring for someone. Struggling to feed points to worry about not having enough to give, while feeling drained suggests that those who depend on you are taking more than you can spare. Feeding an adult or an animal is read as care flowing in an unexpected direction. It can help to notice who or what you are nourishing right now.
