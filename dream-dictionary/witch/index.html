Witch in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

The witch is read as feminine power, transformation and hidden knowledge. Tradition sees both healing and threat in the figure, depending on how she appears. A kind witch brewing herbs is read as wisdom, intuition and the ability to change things quietly; a frightening one as fear of someone’s influence, or of your own anger. Being a witch yourself is taken as owning a power you have kept hidden. A witch flying on a broom is read as independence and the wish to break free of rules. Being cursed by one points to feeling controlled or blamed. The dream often asks how you relate to strength that does not fit expectations.
