Child driving a car in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A child behind the wheel is read as inexperience or impulsiveness steering a serious situation. It can also stand for your inner child taking control — playful, but not yet ready for the road.

A question for you

Has this come up in your dreams before? What was going on in your life then?
