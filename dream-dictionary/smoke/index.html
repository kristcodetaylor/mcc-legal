Smoke in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Smoke is read as a sign that something is burning out of sight, and as confusion that hides the source of a problem. Seeing smoke rise from a house is usually read as worry about home or family. Being surrounded by it is linked to feeling unable to see clearly or breathe freely, while smoke clearing points to understanding returning. Smoke signals stand for messages that are hard to read, and a thin curl of smoke from a chimney for warmth and life inside. It can help to notice where something seems to be smouldering in your days.
