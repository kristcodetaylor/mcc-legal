Cow or bull in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

The cow is traditionally a symbol of nourishment, patience and plenty — the animal that feeds the household. A healthy, well-fed cow is read as security and the comfort of home. A thin or sick cow is linked to worry about resources or about someone who gives too much. Milking a cow points to drawing on what you have built up. The bull adds stubborn strength, desire and drive; being charged by a bull is read as a force or a temper you are struggling to hold back. Dream books ask where in your life you are feeding others and where you need feeding.
