Losing a child in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Losing sight of a child in a dream is one of the most distressing images, and it is read symbolically, not as a warning. Dream books tie it to fear for someone in your care, or to losing touch with your own sense of wonder and play. Searching a crowd or a shop in panic is linked to feeling stretched too thin to protect everything that matters. Finding the child again is read as reconnection and relief, while a stranger’s child going missing points to a broader worry about responsibility. It can help to notice what in your days feels most precious and least attended.
