Grey hair in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Grey hair is read as wisdom, experience and the passage of time. Finding a single grey hair is usually taken as a first awareness that something in life is maturing or changing. Going grey all at once in a dream points to sudden worry, or to the weight of responsibilities that ages a person from the inside. Seeing a loved one with grey hair can reflect respect for them, or concern for how much they carry. Dream books also read it kindly, as a quiet acceptance of who you are becoming.
