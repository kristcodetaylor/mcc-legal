Tree in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A tree is read as a life: roots for family and past, trunk for strength, branches for where you are growing. Its condition is taken as a picture of your own. A tall, healthy tree points to stability and growth, and a tree full of fruit to reward and abundance. A bare or dead tree is linked to a phase of rest, loss or a link to the past that feels thin. A falling tree is read as a shaken sense of security, while climbing one stands for ambition and new perspective. It can help to notice which part of the tree drew your eye — roots, trunk or branches.
