Work in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Dreams about work are read as duty, standing and the wish to be useful. Tradition treats them mostly as the day’s pressure being worked through at night. Arguing with a boss points to tension around authority, while forgetting a task or arriving unprepared is read as fear of falling short. Being back at an old job is a common version, taken as comparing your life now with your life then. Working endlessly without finishing suggests a weariness of spirit, and a pleasant day at work is read as satisfaction with your role.
