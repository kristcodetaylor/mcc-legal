Blood in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Blood is life force and family tie. Dream books read bleeding as energy draining away, and blood as a sign of how deeply something matters. Seeing a lot of blood is linked to emotional exhaustion or strong passion; bleeding from a wound points to a hurt that has not healed. Someone else bleeding is read as worry for them or guilt about a conflict. Blood ties stand for family and loyalty, sometimes for obligations that weigh heavily. Old traditions linked blood with kinship more than with harm; it can help to notice what in your life is draining or strengthening you.
