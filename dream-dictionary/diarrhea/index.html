Diarrhea in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

An urgent need to let go is read as losing control of a situation, or as feelings rushing out before they have been dealt with. Dream books also tie it to nervousness before an event. Being caught without a toilet points to a fear of embarrassment or of being exposed at a bad moment. Having it in public is read as worry that your stress is visible to everyone. Old folk dream books, oddly, linked the image to money moving quickly, both in and out.
