Bedbug in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Bedbugs invade the place of rest, so they are read as worries that follow you to bed and disturb the one place that should feel safe. Dream books link them to anxieties you cannot quite see but feel all night, and to people or situations that quietly drain you. Finding bedbugs in your own bed is read as unease inside your most private life; in a hotel or a stranger’s bed as worry about trusting new places or people. Trying to get rid of them is taken as the wish to reclaim rest and privacy. Being bitten again and again reflects small, repeated stresses. It can help to ask what you carry to bed each night that does not belong there.
