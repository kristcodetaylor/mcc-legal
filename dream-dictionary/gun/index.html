Gun in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A gun stands for power at a distance and the threat of force, and dream books read it as control, aggression or the fear of being targeted. Holding a gun is read as wanting to protect yourself or take control of a situation. Having a gun pointed at you is taken as feeling threatened, pressured or put on the spot. A gun that will not fire is read as frustration and powerlessness — strong feelings that cannot get out. Hiding a gun is read as suppressed anger. Like all dreams of weapons, it is read symbolically and not as a forecast. It can help to ask where in your life you feel a need to defend yourself.
