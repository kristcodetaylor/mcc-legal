Horse in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

The horse is traditionally a symbol of strength, drive and the body’s own energy. How it moves in the dream — calm, wild, ridden or loose — is read as how that energy feels to you. Riding a horse with ease points to being in control of your direction, while being thrown off is read as a plan or a passion that has run ahead of you. A wild horse galloping free stands for a desire that will not be tamed. A white horse carries the old meaning of nobility and a fresh start, a black one of hidden power. Notice whether you were holding the reins or simply holding on.
