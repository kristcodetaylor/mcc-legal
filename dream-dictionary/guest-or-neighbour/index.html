Guest or neighbour in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A guest is read as something new entering your private space — news, a person, an idea or a change you did not plan. Tradition takes a welcome guest as openness and a warm house as a sign of good company around you. An unwelcome or uninvited guest is read as a boundary being tested, and a neighbour who will not leave as a situation that has overstayed. Preparing for guests who never arrive is linked to expectations that others do not share. Dream books also note that the guest can be a side of you knocking to be let in.
