Bracelet in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A bracelet stands for ties of friendship, love or loyalty, encircling the wrist. Dream books read it as a promise worn, or, when heavy, as a bond that restricts. Receiving a bracelet is usually read as a relationship becoming closer or a commitment being made. A broken bracelet is linked to a bond that feels damaged, while losing one points to fear of drifting apart from someone. Exchanging bracelets with a friend stands for mutual trust and belonging. It can help to notice which ties in your life feel supportive and which feel tight.
