Lake in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A lake is still water held in one place, so tradition reads it as the inner life at rest — a mirror for reflection. Its surface is taken as your mood; its depth as what lies beneath it. A clear, calm lake points to peace, while a dark or murky one suggests uncertainty about your own feelings. Swimming across it is read as working through emotions, and a frozen lake as feelings that are held still. Seeing something rise from the water points to a memory coming back.
