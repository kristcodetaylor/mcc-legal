Octopus in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

The octopus is read as intelligence, adaptability and many demands at once — eight arms reaching in eight directions. Dream books link it to multitasking, to a mind juggling too much, and to the ability to change colour and slip through narrow spaces. Being held by its tentacles is read as feeling entangled in a relationship or obligation that holds on too tightly. An octopus releasing ink is taken as confusion, or as a wish to hide and get away. Watching one move gracefully is read as admiration for flexible, creative thinking. The dream may be asking how many things are pulling at you right now, and which grip you could loosen.
