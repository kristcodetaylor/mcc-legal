Baby in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A baby is the classic symbol of a new beginning — an idea, a project or a side of you that has just arrived and is still fragile. Holding a calm baby is linked to tenderness and hope. A crying baby points to a need, your own or someone else’s, that wants care. Forgetting or losing a baby is a very common anxiety dream, read as fear of neglecting something important amid busy days. Dreaming of a baby when you are not expecting one is usually symbolic rather than literal. Dream books ask what new thing in your life needs nurturing.
