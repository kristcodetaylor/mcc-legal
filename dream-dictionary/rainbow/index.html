Rainbow in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

The rainbow is one of the most hopeful symbols: calm after a storm and a bridge between two worlds. Tradition reads it as reconciliation, a promise of peace after conflict, and as harmony between different parts of life. A bright, full rainbow is linked to relief and a fresh start, while a faint or broken one points to hope that still feels fragile. Walking toward a rainbow is read as chasing something beautiful that keeps its distance. A double rainbow is seen as a doubled blessing. The dream often follows a difficult time.
