Mother-in-law in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A mother-in-law is read as the authority of another family and the wish to be accepted by it. Dream books link her to expectations, boundaries, and comparisons with your own mother. A friendly mother-in-law is read as feeling welcomed and at ease in a new role. Arguing with her, or being criticised, is taken as tension about being judged, or a boundary that needs to be set. A mother-in-law moving into your house is read as outside influence entering your private life. Dreaming of a mother-in-law you do not have is read as worry about fitting into a new group. It can help to notice whose approval you are waiting for right now.
