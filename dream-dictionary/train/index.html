Train in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A train runs on fixed tracks, so tradition reads it as the course others have set, or a path you share with many. Missing it is the classic version, read as fear of a chance slipping past. Being on the wrong train points to a sense that life is heading somewhere that was not your choice, while a train that will not stop is taken as momentum that is hard to break. Waiting on a platform is read as a period of transition. A smooth, comfortable journey is a gentler variant, taken as trust in the direction you are going.
