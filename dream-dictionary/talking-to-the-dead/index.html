Talking to the dead in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Talking with someone who has died is read as grief, love and things left unsaid. Dream books treat it as a gentle way of working through loss, not as a message about anyone’s fate. A warm, calm conversation is read as comfort and acceptance, while an argument points to an unresolved feeling. A loved one giving advice is linked to their values still living in you. Being unable to hear them clearly is read as grief that is still raw. Many people wake from such dreams comforted. It can help to notice what you most wanted to say, and whether it can be said in another way.
