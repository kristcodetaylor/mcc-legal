Red-light district in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A red-light district is read symbolically as desire, temptation and the parts of life kept in the shadows. Dream books treat it as a question about what you want and what you judge in yourself, without condemning the dreamer.

A question for you

Has this come up in your dreams before? What was going on in your life then?
