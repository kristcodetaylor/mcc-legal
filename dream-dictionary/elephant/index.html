Elephant in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

The elephant is a symbol of memory, strength and steadiness, and in many traditions of wisdom and good fortune. Dream books also read it as something big that cannot be ignored any longer — the proverbial elephant in the room. A calm elephant is linked to patience and to support from someone strong, while a charging or angry one points to a large force or a feeling that has been held in too long. A baby elephant stands for loyalty and family bonds. Riding one is read as gaining command over something powerful.
