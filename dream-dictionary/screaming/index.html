Screaming in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Screaming is read as feeling that needs a way out — fear, anger or pain that has been held in. A scream with no sound is a classic dream, traditionally about not being heard or not daring to speak up. Hearing someone else scream is linked to worry for them or to a part of you calling for attention. Screaming in fear points to an anxiety close to the surface, and screaming in anger to a boundary crossed. Waking from your own scream is read as release. It can help to ask what you have wanted to say loudly but kept quiet.
