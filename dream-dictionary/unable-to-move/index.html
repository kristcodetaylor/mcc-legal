Unable to move in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Being unable to move or cry out is a very common dream. It is traditionally read as feeling powerless in a waking situation, with your will and your options out of step. Legs that will not run while something approaches is the classic version, read as a problem you feel unable to escape or to tackle. A voice that makes no sound points to something that has not yet been said. Many cultures also have old names for the sense of a heavy presence while lying frozen on waking; dream books treat it as a passing state between sleep and waking, not as anything sinister.
