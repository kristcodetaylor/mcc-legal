Forgotten class in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Discovering a class you signed up for and never attended is read as a duty that slipped out of sight while life moved on. Dream books link it to the nagging sense that some part of your life has gone unattended for too long. Realising it on the day of the final exam is read as fear that neglect is about to be noticed, while searching for the classroom points to not knowing where to begin. Adults often dream of school years later, reading it as a current responsibility in an old costume. It can help to ask which small task or relationship has been quietly waiting for you.
