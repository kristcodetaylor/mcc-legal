Bat in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

The bat finds its way in the dark, so it is read as intuition and as fears that come out at night. Tradition also links it to passage from one state to another — the old self giving way to a new one. A bat flying at you is read as an anxiety that feels sudden and hard to catch, and bats in your house as a worry that has settled close to home. In some cultures the bat is a sign of good fortune and long life. Hanging upside down, it may ask you to look at a situation from the opposite side.
