Walls closing in in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Walls moving inward are read as pressure building and options narrowing. Dream books ask what situation is leaving you less and less room. A room shrinking slowly is linked to stress that grows unnoticed, while walls that close suddenly point to a deadline or decision arriving all at once. Pushing back against them is read as resistance and the wish to set limits. Finding an opening at the last moment is read as a solution still within reach. The dream often appears when life feels crowded with duties. It can help to ask where you could make even a little more space.
