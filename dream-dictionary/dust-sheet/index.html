Dust sheet in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Furniture under dust sheets stands for a part of life put on hold or closed off. Tradition reads lifting the sheets as returning to something left untouched for a while.

A question for you

Has this come up in your dreams before? What was going on in your life then?
