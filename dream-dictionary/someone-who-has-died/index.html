Someone who has died in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Dreaming of someone who has died is one of the most common dreams of all. Tradition reads it as memory and unfinished conversation — the bond continuing in another form. A peaceful, smiling loved one is linked to comfort, reassurance and acceptance. A deceased person who speaks is read as your own inner voice using a familiar face. If they seem sad or distant, dream books see grief still being worked through; seeing them alive again points to longing and love that has not ended. It is not a sign of anything to come; it can simply help to notice what you wish you could still say.
