Tooth extraction in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Having a tooth pulled is traditionally read as removing something that has become a source of pain. Dream books connect it to a necessary loss — a habit, a tie or a role you are ready to let go of. Pulling your own tooth points to deciding to end something yourself, while a dentist doing it suggests letting others help with a hard step. Feeling relief afterwards is read as freedom from an old ache. Many folk books also tie teeth to family, so the dream may echo worry about someone close.
