Shop or market in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A shop is read as choice and worth: what you want, and what you are ready to give for it. Empty shelves traditionally stand for options that feel few, while a full, bright shop is linked to opportunity and plenty. Being unable to afford something points to a sense of limits, and shoplifting to taking what you feel you are not given. A busy market is read as social exchange and the bustle of daily life. Getting lost in a large store stands for too many choices. The dream may ask what you are really shopping for in waking life.
