Bullet in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A bullet is read as a sharp, sudden word or event that strikes from a distance, and dodging one as narrowly escaping a hurt you saw coming. Dream books link it to criticism, a harsh message or news that lands quickly. Finding a bullet is read as noticing a tension before it erupts. Holding bullets in your hand is taken as having the means to hurt or to protect, and choosing which. A bullet that misses is read as relief and a lucky escape from a difficult moment. Many bullets flying are read as a chaotic period full of conflict. The dream describes feelings of exposure, not anything that lies ahead.
