Forest in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

The forest is the classic place of the unknown. Dream books read it as the unexplored part of yourself — a place where you can lose the path, and find something too. Being lost in a dark forest is linked to confusion or to a phase without clear direction. A sunlit forest is read as growth, peace and a return to nature; being chased through the trees points to fears you have not yet named. Finding a clearing stands for sudden understanding; in folk tales the forest is where tests and helpers appear. Dream books ask whether the forest felt frightening or welcoming.
