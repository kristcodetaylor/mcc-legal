Child in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A child is read as innocence, potential and the part of you that is still growing. It can also point to something that needs care and patience. A happy, playing child is linked to joy, creativity and a lighter way of being. A crying or lost child is read as a neglected need or a vulnerable part of yourself. Being a child again points to nostalgia or to feeling small in a situation. Caring for a child stands for responsibility and nurture; it can help to notice what part of you has been asking for attention.
