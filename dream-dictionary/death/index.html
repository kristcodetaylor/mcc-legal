Death in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Death in a dream is almost never read literally, and a dream about dying is not a notice of anyone’s death. Tradition reads it as an ending that makes room — a habit, a role or a period of life coming to a close. Dreaming of your own death is one of the most common dreams of all and is taken as a big change in how you see yourself. The death of someone close is read as a shift in the relationship, or as fear of losing them, rather than as anything about their health. Dream books often add that what follows the death in the dream — calm, grief, a new scene — says the most.
