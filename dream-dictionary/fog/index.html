Fog in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Fog stands for uncertainty and confusion — not being able to see far ahead. Tradition reads walking through it as moving forward without the full picture, trusting the next step. Being lost in thick fog is usually read as feeling unsure of a decision or of someone’s intentions. Fog lifting to reveal a landscape is a classic image of clarity returning after a confused time. Driving through it stands for trying to keep control while information is missing. Dream books also see fog as a gentle veil, giving time before something has to be faced. It can help to ask what in your life feels hard to see clearly.
