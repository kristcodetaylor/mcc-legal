Nun in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A nun stands for devotion and a life set apart. Tradition reads the figure as a search for peace, or as restraint placed on desire. A kind nun is linked to care, patience and quiet guidance, while a strict one points to rules you feel bound by. Becoming a nun yourself is read as a wish for retreat, simplicity or a deeper commitment. A convent or cloister is read as inner sanctuary. The image is read with respect for the faiths that honour such a life. It can help to notice where you would like more stillness or more freedom.
