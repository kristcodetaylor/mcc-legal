Failure in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Dreams of failing are among the most common and are read as a fear of falling short, not as a verdict. They tend to appear when the stakes of something feel high. Failing an exam is linked to fear of being judged, failing at work to worries about your place, and letting someone down to the weight of others’ expectations. Calmly accepting the failure in the dream is read as growing self-trust, while panic points to strong pressure on yourself. Trying again after failing is read as resilience. It can help to ask whose standard you are measuring yourself against.
