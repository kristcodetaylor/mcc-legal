Zoo in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

The zoo is read as wildness kept behind bars — instincts held in check and put on display. Dream books also link it to feeling watched, or to a world that seems orderly but confined. An escaped animal points to a feeling or urge breaking free, while being inside a cage yourself suggests feeling trapped or observed. Visiting a zoo with children is read as curiosity and a wish to show others the world. It can help to ask which part of you feels kept behind glass.
