Hugging in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Hugging or cuddling is read as warmth, comfort and closeness. Tradition takes it as the need for affection, or as reconciliation with someone — or with a part of yourself. Hugging someone you have lost touch with is linked to the wish to mend the bond, while hugging a stranger points to openness to new connection. An awkward or cold hug is read as distance in a relationship. Being hugged tightly by someone who has died is read as comfort in grief. It can help to notice who you would like to be closer to, and what keeps you apart.
