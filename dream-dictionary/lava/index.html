Lava in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Lava is read as intense emotion in motion — destructive, but also creating new land as it cools. Dream books link it to passion and anger that reshape whatever they touch. Lava creeping toward you is usually read as a feeling you can no longer keep at a distance, and crossing it safely as getting through a heated situation intact. Glowing lava seen at night is often tied to fascination with your own strength or desire. Hardened, cooled lava stands for a storm of feeling that has passed and left solid ground behind. It can help to ask where your energy is running hot right now.
