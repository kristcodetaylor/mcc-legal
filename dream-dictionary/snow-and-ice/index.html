Snow and ice in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Snow is read as stillness, purity and a pause, when everything is covered and quiet. Ice can stand for feelings that have gone cold or been put on hold. Fresh snow falling is linked to a clean slate and a calm new start. Being stuck in snow points to delay, or to plans frozen for now; playing in snow is read as a return to childhood lightness. Walking on thin ice stands for a risky situation or a fragile relationship, while melting snow is read as feelings thawing and life starting to move again. Dream books ask what in your life is waiting under the white.
