Cancer in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Cancer in a dream is traditionally read as a fear or resentment growing quietly inside, or a situation that is spreading beyond your control. Being told you have it is usually taken as shock at something you suddenly have to face, while a loved one with the illness points to worry for them or fear of losing them. Recovering in the dream is read as hope that a heavy matter can be resolved. It is a symbol of worry, never a diagnosis or a forecast. It can help to ask what has been eating at you lately.
