Honeymoon in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A honeymoon is read as the sweet, early stage of anything new — a bond, a job, a home. Tradition also sees it as a wish to pause and enjoy a commitment before everyday life begins. A sunny, happy honeymoon is read as delight and closeness; a trip that goes wrong, with lost luggage or arguments, as worry that the good feeling will not last. Being on a honeymoon alone is taken as a need for rest and attention to yourself. Dreaming of a honeymoon long after the wedding is read as missing early lightness in a relationship. It can help to notice where in your life you would like more time simply to enjoy what you have chosen.
