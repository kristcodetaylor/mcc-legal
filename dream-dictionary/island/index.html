Island in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

An island is read as solitude: a refuge when it is chosen, isolation when it is not. Dream books ask which of the two it felt like; being stranded on a deserted island points to loneliness or to feeling cut off from support. A sunny, beautiful island is linked to the wish for escape, rest and a simpler life. Sailing toward an island stands for a goal or a new beginning in sight. Watching your island being surrounded by rising water is read as feeling your private space shrink. It can help to notice where in your days you long for distance — or for connection.

A question for you
