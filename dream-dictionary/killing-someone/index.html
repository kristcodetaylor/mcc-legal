Killing someone in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Taking a life in a dream shocks many dreamers, yet tradition reads it almost entirely as a symbol: ending a habit, a role or a part of yourself you want to be rid of. It says nothing about who the dreamer is or wants to be. Killing a stranger is read as cutting off an unknown trait or a pressure you cannot name. Killing someone you know is taken as anger towards them, or the wish for a dynamic with them to end. Killing in self-defence is read as protecting your boundaries. Feeling guilt in the dream reflects a conscience at work. It can help to ask what you want to stop, and how it might end peacefully.
