Car in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A car is read as the way you steer your life. Who is driving matters: at the wheel you are in charge; as a passenger, you have handed someone else the direction. Failing brakes are one of the most common car dreams and are traditionally read as a situation moving faster than you can control, not as a warning about the road. A crash points to fear of a clash or of a plan going wrong, while a stolen or lost car is read as a loss of independence. Driving smoothly on an open road is taken as confidence and freedom.
