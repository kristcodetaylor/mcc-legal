Engagement in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

An engagement is read as a promise and a turning point: a decision made, with the commitment still ahead. Dream books link it to readiness for a new bond, with another person or with a goal. Getting engaged happily is read as confidence in a direction; feeling unsure or trapped as doubt about a choice already in motion. A broken engagement is taken as hesitation or a wish to keep options open, not as anything foretold. An engagement ring that is lost or does not fit is read as unease with what the commitment asks of you. It can help to notice which promises in your waking life are waiting to be kept, and how you feel about them.
