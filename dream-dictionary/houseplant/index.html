Houseplant in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A plant kept indoors depends entirely on the care it is given, so dream books read it as something in your life that grows only as far as your attention lets it — a project, a bond or your own wellbeing.

A question for you

Has this come up in your dreams before? What was going on in your life then?
