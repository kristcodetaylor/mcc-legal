Cousin in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A cousin is read as someone close in age and history, yet with a life of their own. Dream books see cousins as a mirror for comparing your choices and remembering shared childhood. Playing with cousins as children is read as nostalgia for freedom and simple belonging. Arguing with a cousin is taken as rivalry, or a family tension that has never been spoken. A cousin you have not seen for years appearing in a dream is read as part of your past asking to be remembered. A successful or troubled cousin is often read as a version of the life you might have lived. It can help to notice what you envy or worry about in them.
