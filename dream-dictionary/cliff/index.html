Cliff in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A cliff edge stands for a decisive moment, a point where the familiar ends abruptly. Dream books read it as risk and the need to choose your next step carefully. Standing calmly at the edge is linked to readiness for change, while being pushed toward it points to pressure from others. Hanging from a cliff is read as clinging to something that is slipping away. Jumping and flying is read as a leap of faith that feels freeing. Climbing a cliff points to effort and ambition. It can help to notice which decision in your life feels like standing on the edge.
