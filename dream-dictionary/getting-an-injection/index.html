Getting an injection in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Receiving an injection is read as something being put into you from outside — help, influence or change you did not fully choose. Dream books also see in it a hard moment accepted for the sake of healing. Fear of the needle points to dread of a small pain that stands between you and something better. Giving someone an injection is read as wanting to fix or influence them. Being injected against your will suggests feeling that others are pushing their ideas into your life.

A question for you

Has this come up in your dreams before? What was going on in your life then?
