Bee in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Bees are traditionally a good sign of shared work, order and reward for effort. A buzzing hive is read as a busy, productive time and a sense of belonging to a community. A bee sting is read as a sharp word or a small hurt that stays with you. A swarm of bees can stand for being overwhelmed by tasks or by other people’s expectations. Honey alongside bees adds the old meaning of sweetness earned by work, while dead bees are linked to tiredness and effort that feels wasted. Dream books ask whether your own labour feels shared and rewarded right now.
