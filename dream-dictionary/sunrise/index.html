Sunrise in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Watching the sun rise is one of the clearest images of a new beginning. It is traditionally read as hope, renewal and clarity after a long or troubled night. Seeing it from a mountaintop or over the sea is usually read as perspective and a sense of possibility opening up. A sunrise that follows a frightening part of the dream is linked to relief and the end of a difficult phase. Watching it with someone stands for a shared fresh start, while a hazy, slow dawn suggests change arriving gently. It can help to notice what in your days feels like it is just beginning.
