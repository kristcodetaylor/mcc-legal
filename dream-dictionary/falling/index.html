Falling in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Falling is one of the most common dreams. It is traditionally read as loss of control or support — a worry that something you stand on will not hold. Falling from a height points to fear of failing or losing status. Jerking awake as you fall is linked to stress at the end of a long day, while falling slowly or gently is read as letting go and surrendering. Being pushed stands for feeling undermined by someone, and landing safely for resilience. Dream books ask where in your waking life you feel unsupported.
