Hospital in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A hospital is read as the need to heal and to be looked after. Dream books treat it as a sign that something in you is asking for rest and repair, often emotional rather than physical. Being a patient points to letting others care for you, while visiting someone is read as concern for them or for what they represent. Working in a hospital is taken as being the one who holds everyone together. A dark, empty ward is a more anxious version, read as worry with nowhere to go. The dream is not a statement about health; it asks where you need gentleness.
