Building or repairing in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Building is read as making something that lasts — a home, a career, a relationship. Repairing traditionally stands for mending what was damaged. Laying foundations is linked to new beginnings and patient groundwork, while a building that keeps collapsing points to doubt about your efforts or support. Working on a house that never gets finished is read as a long project still in progress. Fixing something broken stands for reconciliation or recovery. The dream may ask what in your life you are carefully putting together, brick by brick.
