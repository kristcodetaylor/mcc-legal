Alcohol in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Alcohol is read in dreams as escape and loosened control — a wish to relax, forget or celebrate, or a worry about losing your grip. Being drunk is usually linked to letting go of restraint or to saying what is normally held back. Watching someone else drink too much points to concern for them, or to a habit around you that feels unsteady. Refusing a drink is read as holding on to clarity and to your own limits. The image describes a feeling, not a judgement, and it can help to ask what the dream wanted to escape from or to celebrate.
