Haunted house in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A haunted house is a home where the past will not stay quiet. Dream tradition reads it as memories, regrets or old relationships still taking up space in the present. Hearing noises in another room points to something you sense but have not looked at, while meeting the ghost suggests being ready to face it. Living in a haunted house is read as an atmosphere at home that feels heavy. It can help to notice what old story still echoes in your days.
