Secret room in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Finding a secret room is one of the classic dreams of discovery. It is read as a hidden talent, a forgotten memory or a side of yourself you are only now allowed to see. A bright, beautiful room points to untapped potential, while a dusty or dark one suggests something long neglected. Finding someone already inside is read as an unknown part of your life coming into view. Dream books see it as an invitation to grow beyond the rooms you usually live in.
