Inheritance in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

An inheritance is read as what you carry from those before you — traits, values, burdens or gifts. Dream books take it as legacy and identity rather than as anything to do with real money. Receiving a large inheritance is read as recognising strengths you were handed without earning them. Inheriting a house is taken as stepping into a family role; inheriting debts or a broken object as a burden passed down that you did not choose. Quarrelling over an inheritance is read as rivalry about who is valued most. Refusing it is taken as wanting to make your own way. The dream asks what you have been given, and what you want to do with it.
