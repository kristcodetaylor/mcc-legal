Recklessness in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Throwing caution aside in a dream speaks of a wish to break free of control — or a worry about where that wish could lead. Dream books read it as a tension between freedom and care.

A question for you

Has this come up in your dreams before? What was going on in your life then?
