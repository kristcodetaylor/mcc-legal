Own funeral in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Watching your own funeral is read as the end of an old identity and a look at how others see you. It is a dream of transformation and self-reflection, not a forecast of anything. Seeing people mourn you is linked to wanting to know you matter, while an empty funeral points to feeling unseen. Feeling calm or relieved is read as readiness to leave an old role behind. Speaking at your own funeral is read as taking stock of your life. It can help to ask which version of yourself is ending, and what you want to carry into the next one.
