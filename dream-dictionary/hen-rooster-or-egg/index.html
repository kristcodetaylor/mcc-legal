Hen, rooster or egg in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

The hen is read as home, care and the small cares of everyday life, while the rooster stands for pride, confidence and a wake-up call. The egg is the classic symbol of something not hatched yet — a plan, a hope, a possibility. Finding eggs is traditionally linked to new beginnings, and a broken egg to a plan that feels fragile or disappointed. A rooster crowing is read as a signal to stop sleeping on something. Chicks around a hen point to protectiveness and to whatever you are raising with patience.
