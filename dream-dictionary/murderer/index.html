Murderer in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A murderer in a dream is read symbolically, as a force that ends something — a relationship, a phase, a part of the self. Tradition takes it as fear, or as the wish for something to stop, and never as a literal threat. Being chased by a murderer is read as running from a problem that feels deadly serious. Being the murderer yourself is taken as anger or a wish to cut something out of your life. A murderer you know is read as tension or distrust towards that person. A faceless murderer reflects anxiety without a clear source. It can help to ask what in your life feels as if it threatens your peace.
