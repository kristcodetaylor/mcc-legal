Hair loss in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Hair falling out is one of the classic anxiety dreams, read as a fear of losing control, attractiveness or vitality. Hair coming out in handfuls is usually linked to stress that feels like it is slipping through your fingers, while a few strands on a brush point to smaller, steady worries. Watching it fall out in front of others is read as a fear of being seen at a weak moment. It reflects strain rather than anything about your body. It can help to notice where in your days you feel you are losing your grip.

A question for you
