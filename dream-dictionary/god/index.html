God in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Dreaming of God is read across many traditions as meeting the highest values you hold — meaning, conscience, a sense of order. How the presence feels, near or distant, kind or stern, is taken as how that relationship feels now. Hearing a voice is linked to an inner sense of guidance, while feeling abandoned points to doubt or loneliness. Speaking with God is read as searching for answers or forgiveness. Each faith gives the image its own shape, and dream books treat it with respect. It can help to notice what question you carried into the dream.
