Wind in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Wind is read as change and as forces you cannot see but can feel. A gentle wind stands for fresh thoughts, a strong one for being pushed by events. Walking against the wind points to effort against resistance or other people’s opinions. Being lifted or carried by the wind is linked to surrender, sometimes to freedom. A storm wind tearing things away is read as upheaval that clears space for something new. Whispering wind in folk tradition carries news or rumours; dream books ask which way the wind was pushing you, and whether you wanted to go there.
