Missed call in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A missed call is read as an opportunity or a person who reached out at the wrong moment. Dream tradition links it to regret, and to wondering what might have been said. Seeing many missed calls is usually read as feeling overwhelmed by others’ demands, or guilty about not being available. A missed call from someone who has died is linked to longing and unfinished conversations. Calling back without an answer points to frustration about reconnecting. It can help to ask whose voice you have been missing lately.
