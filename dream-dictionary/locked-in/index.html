Locked in in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Being shut in a room you cannot leave is read as feeling boxed in by a situation, a rule or a role. Tradition notes whether someone else turned the key or whether the door was yours to open.

A question for you

Has this come up in your dreams before? What was going on in your life then?
