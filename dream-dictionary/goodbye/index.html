Goodbye in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A goodbye is read as letting go — of a person, a place or an old self. Being left behind traditionally stands for the fear of not being chosen. Saying goodbye calmly is linked to acceptance and a chapter closing well, while a tearful farewell points to grief or a bond that still matters. A goodbye to someone who has died is a common, gentle dream, often read as peace with the loss. Being unable to say goodbye stands for something unfinished. The dream may ask what in your life is ready to be released.
