Homeless person in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Meeting a homeless person, or being one in a dream, is read as feeling without a secure place — at home, in work or in relationships. It speaks of vulnerability and the wish for shelter. Being homeless yourself is linked to fear of losing stability, or to a period of change when nothing feels settled. Helping a homeless person is read as compassion and the wish to care for a neglected part of yourself. Walking past someone without stopping can point to a need you are ignoring. It can help to ask where you feel most at home now, and what makes you feel safe.
