Anger in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Anger is read as a boundary that was crossed and energy that was held back. Dream books take it as strength looking for a fair use. Shouting at someone you love is linked to tension that is hard to voice in waking life, while being the target of someone’s anger points to fear of conflict or disapproval. Uncontrolled rage is read as pressure building up for too long. Calmly expressing anger stands for healthy self-respect. It can help to notice who or what the anger was aimed at, because that often shows where the strain lies.
