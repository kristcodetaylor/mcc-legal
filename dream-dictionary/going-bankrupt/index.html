Going bankrupt in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Bankruptcy in a dream is read as feeling that your reserves — of energy, confidence or patience — have run dry. It speaks of depletion and worth, not of what is in the bank. Watching your possessions being taken away is linked to fear of losing control over your life, while confessing bankruptcy to someone points to shame about not coping. Feeling relief after losing everything is read as freedom from a burden. Starting over in the dream is read as resilience. It can help to notice where you have been giving more than you have.
