Fire in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Fire is both passion and destruction. Tradition reads a warm, contained fire as vitality and home; a fire out of control as anger, or as change that clears the old away. A house on fire is one of the most common variants and is usually read as intense emotion or upheaval in your private life, not as a literal danger. Being burned points to a situation or relationship that hurt you. Putting out a fire is linked to calming a conflict, while lighting one is read as starting something new. It can help to notice what in your days feels heated — and whether that warmth comforts or consumes.
