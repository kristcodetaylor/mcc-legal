Menstruation in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Menstruation is read as cycles, renewal and release — the end of one phase and the start of the next. Bleeding unexpectedly in front of others points to fear of embarrassment or of something private being seen. A period arriving when it should not is linked to worry about timing and plans. For men, dreaming of it is read as contact with cycles and feelings they rarely voice. It is a symbol of change and natural rhythm, not a sign about the body.
