Bird in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Birds are read as freedom, news and thoughts that rise above the everyday. A bird in flight speaks of perspective and of plans taking wing; a caged one of a wish held back or a talent kept small. Birdsong is traditionally linked to good news and lightness of mood. A dead or injured bird is read as a hope that feels grounded for now, not as an omen of loss. A bird flying into your home is an old folk image of a message arriving. It can help to notice which thought in your waking life keeps trying to take off.
