Drink in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Drinking is read as taking something in — feeling, comfort, celebration. Thirst stands for a need unmet, and clear, cool water for refreshment and renewal. Drunkenness is traditionally linked to the wish to let go of control or to escape pressure for a while. Being offered a drink points to welcome or temptation, depending on how it felt. A drink that tastes bitter or strange is read as an experience hard to accept. Toasting with others stands for shared joy. The dream may ask what you have been thirsty for in waking life.
