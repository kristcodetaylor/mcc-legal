Darkness in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Total darkness is read as the unknown, a place where you cannot yet see your way. Tradition treats it as fear and uncertainty, but also as the fertile dark from which new things emerge. Feeling your way through a dark room is usually read as making decisions without full information. Being afraid of something hidden in the darkness is linked to worries that grow larger because they stay unnamed. Finding a light, a door or a hand is a classic image of help or insight arriving, while resting peacefully in the dark points to retreat and recovery. It can help to ask which part of life feels unclear right now.
