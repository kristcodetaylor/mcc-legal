Orange in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Bright and full of sun, the orange is read as vitality, generosity and warmth shared with others. Peeled and divided, it speaks of a friendship or a joy that is easy to share. Picking oranges from a tree is usually read as enjoying good energy and the results of effort. A sour or dry orange is linked to disappointment, while squeezing one for juice points to making the most of what you have. It can help to notice where warmth is flowing in your days.
