Breaking in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Something breaking is read as a loss, a release or a change that cannot be undone. Dream books ask whether it felt like damage or like relief. Breaking glass or dishes is linked to tension snapping or to old patterns ending, while breaking a precious object points to fear of harming something you value. Breaking free from chains or walls is read as liberation. Someone breaking your things stands for a feeling of being disrespected. Breaking a rule carries the image of testing limits. It can help to notice what felt fragile before you went to sleep.

A question for you

Has this come up in your dreams before? What was going on in your life then?
