School in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

School is read as the place of lessons and comparison. Being back there as an adult is a classic dream, traditionally about being tested or measured again in some area of life. Not finding your classroom points to feeling lost in a new role, while sitting among old classmates is read as revisiting the person you were then and how others saw you. Being unprepared for a lesson is taken as fear that you have missed something everyone else seems to know. A pleasant school dream, oddly, is often read as readiness to learn something new.
