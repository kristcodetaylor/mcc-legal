Moving house in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Moving house is read as a change of inner address: leaving one way of living for another. It traditionally stands for a new stage starting. Packing boxes is linked to sorting what to keep and what to let go, while an unfamiliar new home points to adapting to change. Being forced to move is read as change that feels imposed rather than chosen. Moving back to an old home stands for nostalgia or a return to old habits. It can help to notice how the new place felt — open and bright, or cramped and strange.
