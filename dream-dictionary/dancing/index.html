Dancing in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Dancing is read as joy, freedom in the body and being in step with someone. Tradition takes it as a sign of ease with life’s rhythm. Dancing with a partner is linked to harmony in a relationship, while stepping on toes points to small misunderstandings. Dancing alone is read as self-expression and confidence, and dancing in front of a crowd as the wish to be seen. Being unable to dance or forgetting the steps stands for feeling out of sync. A wild dance carries the image of release after holding back for too long.
