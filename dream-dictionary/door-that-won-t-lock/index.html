Door that won’t lock in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A lock that keeps failing is read as feeling unable to protect your private space or set a boundary. Dream books link it to someone or something that keeps getting in despite your efforts.

A question for you

Has this come up in your dreams before? What was going on in your life then?
