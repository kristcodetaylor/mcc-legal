Poisoning in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Being poisoned is read as a harmful influence working on you from within — words, habits or a relationship that are slowly doing damage. Not knowing who poisoned you points to vague distrust, while a known poisoner suggests a specific person you feel wary of. Poisoning someone else is read as guilt, or as resentment you have not expressed openly. Finding an antidote is taken as recovery and clarity. It can help to notice what leaves you feeling worse each time you meet it.
