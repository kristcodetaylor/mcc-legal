Pit bull in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

The pit bull is read as tenacity and strong loyalty, and also as how others judge a reputation. Dream tradition links it to inner force and to being misread from the outside — strength that looks dangerous but may simply be protective. A friendly pit bull is read as a powerful ally, or as your own determination on your side. An aggressive one, or one that bites, is taken as anger or a conflict that has locked its jaws and will not let go. Being afraid of a pit bull that does nothing is read as prejudice or fear outrunning the facts. The dream can ask where you hold on fiercely, and whether that grip still serves you.
