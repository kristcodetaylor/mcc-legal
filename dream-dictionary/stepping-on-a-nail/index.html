Stepping on a nail in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Stepping on a nail is read as a sudden, sharp setback that stops you in your tracks. Tradition links it to a small hurt that demands attention before you can go on.

A question for you

Has this come up in your dreams before? What was going on in your life then?
