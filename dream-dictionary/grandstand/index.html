Grandstand in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

From the grandstand you watch rather than play, so it is traditionally read as observing life from the sidelines. It can stand for support given to others, or for a wish to step onto the field.

A question for you

Does the traditional reading fit your dream, or did it mean something else to you?
