Ancestor in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

An ancestor in a dream is read as the voice of your roots — wisdom, warnings or traits handed down. Folk tradition sees such visits as a reminder of the strength behind you. An ancestor speaking to you is read as guidance drawn from family memory, or your own deeper knowledge borrowing a familiar face. A silent or distant ancestor is taken as questions about heritage that have not been answered. Seeing a line of ancestors is read as belonging and continuity. Many cultures treat these dreams with respect and warmth. It can help to ask which family habits you want to keep, and which you are ready to set down.
