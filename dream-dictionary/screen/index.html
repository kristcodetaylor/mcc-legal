Screen in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A screen is read as watching life from a distance instead of living it — and as the flood of the day’s images being sorted at night. Seeing yourself on a screen is linked to feeling observed or to stepping back to look at your own story. A screen that will not turn on points to frustrated communication, while a cracked one is read as a distorted view. Being unable to look away stands for a pull you find hard to resist. Dream books also read what plays on the screen as a message about what occupies your mind most.
