Key in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

The key is the symbol of access and answers. Finding one is traditionally read as a solution coming within reach; losing one, as feeling shut out. A key that does not fit the lock points to the right effort in the wrong place, or an approach that needs changing. Being handed a key by someone is read as trust, responsibility or a new role being offered. A ring of many keys suggests several options at once, and a broken key a plan that has stalled. Dream books often ask which door in your life the key belongs to.
