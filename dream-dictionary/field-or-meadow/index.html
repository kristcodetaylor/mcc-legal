Field or meadow in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

An open field is read as freedom, room to grow and work that bears fruit. A green meadow is one of the old good signs of ease, rest and a quiet mind. A ploughed field is linked to preparation and to effort that has not yet shown results, while a field of ripe grain points to harvest and reward. A dry or burnt field is read as exhaustion or a plan that ran out of care. Running across a wide field stands for the wish to break free from limits. Dream books also read the edge of a field as a threshold to something new.
