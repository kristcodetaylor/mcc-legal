Cockroach in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

The cockroach is read as survival against all odds, and also as something unwanted that keeps coming back however often it is swept away. Dream books link it to what you would rather not see in your own house — a habit, a worry or a mess left in a dark corner. One cockroach running across the floor is usually read as a small nuisance you keep noticing; a whole infestation as a problem that has grown while it was ignored. Failing to kill it, or watching it return, is taken as frustration with something that resists every quick fix. It can help to ask what in your days feels persistent, unwelcome and harder to clear than it should be.
