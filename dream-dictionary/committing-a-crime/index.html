Committing a crime in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Breaking the law in a dream is read as testing a boundary — a rule you feel bound by, or a wish you do not allow yourself. Dream books treat it as a picture of inner tension, never as a judgement of the dreamer. Fear of being caught is linked to guilt about something small, while feeling thrilled points to a craving for freedom. Being forced into a crime is read as pressure from others. Getting away with it is read as a wish to escape consequences. It can help to notice which rule in your life feels too tight.
