Man in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

An unknown man is traditionally read as the acting, deciding side of life — will, drive, protection. How he behaves is taken as how that force feels to you right now. A man who helps or guides points to confidence and support, while a threatening man in the house or following you is one of the most common anxious versions, read as pressure or an intrusion that feels hard to stop. A man you know is read as your bond with him or the quality he represents. A faceless man suggests a force not yet recognised. The figure belongs to every dreamer, whatever their gender.
