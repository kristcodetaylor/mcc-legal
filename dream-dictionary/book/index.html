Book in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A book is read as knowledge, memory and your own story. Tradition sees a book you cannot read as an answer that is close but not clear yet. Writing a book points to shaping your life story or a project of your own, while an old or forgotten book is read as wisdom from the past coming back. A book with blank pages suggests a chapter not yet written. Searching a library shelf is taken as looking for guidance. Closing a book, gently or with relief, is read as finishing a period of life.
