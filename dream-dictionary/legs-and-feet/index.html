Legs and feet in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Legs and feet are read as your footing and your ability to move ahead. Weak or heavy legs traditionally stand for a step you hesitate to take, and being unable to run for feeling held back by something you cannot name. A hurt or broken leg is linked to a loss of support or independence, while strong legs point to confidence and stability. Bare feet are read as closeness to the ground and to simple truths. Dream books also note that feet show direction, so it can help to notice where in your life you want to go next.
