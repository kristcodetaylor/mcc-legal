Being late in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Being late or missing something is traditionally read as fear of a lost chance, and as pressure — the sense that life is asking for more than there is time for. Running for a bus or train that pulls away is the classic version, read as worry that an opportunity is slipping by. Arriving late to an exam or a wedding points to fear of letting others down at an important moment. Endless small obstacles — lost keys, the wrong shoes, a road that keeps turning — are taken as feeling held back by details. It can help to ask where your days feel overbooked, and what could wait.
