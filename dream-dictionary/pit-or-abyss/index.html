Pit or abyss in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A pit is read as a fear of sinking — into sadness, debt, an old habit or a mistake. Standing at its edge traditionally stands for a risk you can already see, and the dream asks whether you step back in time. Falling into a pit is linked to a sudden loss of footing, while climbing out of one is read as recovery and slow, hard-won strength. Someone else trapped in a pit points to worry for them. A bottomless abyss stands for the unknown itself. It can help to notice which situation in waking life feels like it has no floor.
