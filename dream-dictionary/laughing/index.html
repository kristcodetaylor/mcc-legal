Laughing in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Laughter is read as relief and joy, and sometimes as a cover for unease. Tradition asks who is laughing, and at what. Laughing freely with others is read as ease and belonging, a sign of tension being let go. Being laughed at is one of the more common anxious variants and is traditionally taken as fear of ridicule or of being exposed. Laughing at something that is not funny, or being unable to stop, points to a feeling that has found the wrong door out — nervousness dressed up as cheer.
