Dentures in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

False teeth are read as a front you put on, or as worry about ageing and appearance. Losing them in a dream points to fear of being exposed. Dentures that do not fit suggest saying things that do not feel like your own words. Seeing someone else take out their dentures is read as glimpsing them without their usual mask. Dream books also link the image to confidence that leans on outside support.
