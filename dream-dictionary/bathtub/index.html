Bathtub in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A bathtub is read as relaxation, cleansing and a space to be alone with your thoughts. An overflowing one is taken as emotions that need somewhere to go. A warm, peaceful bath is usually read as healing and self-care after a demanding time. Dirty water is linked to feelings that seem heavy or unresolved, while an empty tub points to a need for comfort that has not been met. Sharing a bath is read as intimacy and trust. It can help to notice whether your days leave you any room to rest.
