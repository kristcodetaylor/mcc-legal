Boss fight in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A boss fight is read as the big challenge at the end of a long stretch, the obstacle everything has been leading up to. Its strength in the dream is taken as how large the waking problem feels.

A question for you

Has this come up in your dreams before? What was going on in your life then?
