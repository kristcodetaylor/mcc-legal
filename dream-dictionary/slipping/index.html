Slipping in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Slipping is read as losing your footing in a situation that seemed stable. Dream books take it as an inner caution — ground that is less firm than it looks, or attention that has wandered. Slipping on ice is linked to emotional coldness or a risky situation, while slipping on stairs points to unsteady progress. Catching yourself before falling is read as quick recovery and resilience. Watching someone else slip is read as concern for them. It can help to notice where you are moving too fast to feel the ground under you.
