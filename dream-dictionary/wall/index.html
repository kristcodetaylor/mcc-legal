Wall in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A wall is read as a boundary: protection from one side, an obstacle from the other. Dream books ask which side of it you are on. Building a wall points to setting limits or shutting something out, while a wall that blocks your path is read as a hurdle you cannot yet see past. Climbing over is taken as determination; a crack or a crumbling wall suggests defences giving way. Walls closing in is a common anxious version, read as feeling pressed by circumstances. Writing on a wall is taken as a message hard to ignore.
