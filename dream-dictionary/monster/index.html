Monster in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A monster is read as a fear given a body. Dream tradition holds that turning to face it — asking what it wants — is how the dream changes. Being chased by a monster is one of the most common nightmares in childhood and adult life, read as avoiding something that grows the more it is avoided. A monster under the bed or in a wardrobe points to a worry kept close and out of sight. Befriending or taming it is taken as accepting a part of yourself that once caused fear. A monster that shrinks when looked at is read as a fear smaller than it seemed.
