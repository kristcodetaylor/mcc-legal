Dead pet in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Seeing a pet that has died is read as a visit of comfort and continued bond. Dream books see it as grief processing, and as unconditional love that remains in memory. A pet that looks healthy and happy is read as peace with the loss, while a pet that seems lost or sick points to grief still unsettled. Playing with it again is linked to longing for its steady companionship. Dreaming that a living pet has died is read as fear of losing comfort, not as a warning. It can help to notice what that animal gave you that you still need.
