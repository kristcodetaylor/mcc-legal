Being wrongly accused in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A false accusation is read as the frustration of being misunderstood. Dream tradition links it to a waking situation where your intentions are not being seen for what they are. Being accused by someone close is linked to a strained trust, while being accused by strangers points to fear of public judgement. Unable to prove innocence is read as powerlessness. Being cleared in the end is read as hope for recognition. It can help to ask where you feel unfairly judged, and who might need to hear your side.
