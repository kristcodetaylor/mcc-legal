Buried alive in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Being buried alive is one of the most frightening dream images, and it is read symbolically as feeling trapped under responsibilities or emotions that press in from all sides. Dream books link it to a need for air, space and voice. Calling out unheard points to feeling ignored, while digging your way out is read as the strength to free yourself. Watching someone else buried is read as worry that a person or a part of you is being smothered. It is an image of pressure, never a forecast. It can help to ask what in your days leaves you no room to breathe.
