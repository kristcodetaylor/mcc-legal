Feast in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A feast is traditionally read as abundance, celebration and belonging. A long table that never seems to empty speaks of fullness, generosity and good company. Being the host is linked to giving and to wanting to bring people together, while being an honoured guest points to recognition. Being left out of a feast, or arriving after everyone has eaten, is read as feeling excluded or overlooked. A feast that tastes of nothing is linked to a success that does not quite satisfy. It can help to notice where in your life you feel nourished by others, and where you are still waiting at the door.
