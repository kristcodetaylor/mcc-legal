Flatline in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A flatline on a monitor is read as energy, feeling or motivation dropping to nothing. Dream books see it as emotional numbness or a project whose life has run out, not as anything about health.

A question for you

Where in your waking life have you met something like this lately?
