Cake in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Cake is the food of occasions. It is traditionally read as celebration, reward and sharing, and a cut cake as how the good things in life are divided. Baking a cake is usually read as preparing something for others or investing in joy. A ruined cake is linked to disappointment or fear of failure at a special moment, while eating cake alone points to quiet self-reward. Being offered a slice stands for being included. It can help to ask what in your life deserves celebrating.
