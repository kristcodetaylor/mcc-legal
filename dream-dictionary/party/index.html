Party in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A party is read as celebration, connection and the wish to belong. Dream books also note that feeling alone at a lively party speaks of distance from those around you. Hosting a party points to a wish to bring people together or to be recognised, while arriving uninvited suggests fear of rejection. A party that turns chaotic is read as social stress. It can help to notice who you were glad to see there and who was missing.
