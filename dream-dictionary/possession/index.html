Possession in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Being taken over by something foreign is read as feeling controlled by emotion, habit or another person. Tradition sees it as a struggle for your own will, not a literal threat. Watching your body act against your wishes is linked to feeling that stress or anger is steering you. Seeing someone else possessed points to a person who seems not themselves. Resisting and regaining control is read as reclaiming autonomy. Dream books across many cultures read it in this symbolic way. It can help to notice what influence in your days feels stronger than your own voice.
