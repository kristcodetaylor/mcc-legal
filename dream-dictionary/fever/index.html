Fever in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A fever is read as intense emotion, passion or inner conflict burning through. It speaks of being consumed by something — and of the heat that comes before a break. Having a high fever is usually linked to anger or desire that is hard to contain, while a fever breaking points to relief after a crisis. Caring for someone with a fever suggests worry about their state of mind. Dream books read it as an emotional temperature, not a sign about the body.
