Shark attack in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A shark attack in a dream is read symbolically as a sudden threat rising from below — a fear, a person or a pressure felt as predatory. Because the shark comes out of deep water, tradition links it to feelings that circle beneath the surface before they strike. Seeing a fin and swimming away is read as sensing trouble early and wanting distance; being pulled under as feeling overwhelmed by something bigger than you. Watching an attack from the shore is taken as anxiety on someone else’s behalf. It describes a feeling, not an event to come, and it can help to ask where in your life you feel watched by something hungry.
