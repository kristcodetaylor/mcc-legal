Being naked in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Being naked in public is a classic dream. It is traditionally read as fear of exposure — and, when it feels free, as the wish to be seen as you are. Feeling ashamed of your nakedness points to vulnerability, insecurity or fear of judgment. Nobody noticing that you are naked is linked to worries that are bigger in your mind than in reality. Being naked in nature stands for innocence and authenticity; seeing someone else naked is read as discovering hidden truths about them. Dream books ask where in your days you feel exposed or unprepared.
