Medicine in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Medicine is read as the search for a remedy and a quick fix. Tradition takes it as a sign that something asks to be healed and not just quietened. Taking medicine in a dream is linked to accepting help, while refusing it points to resistance or doubt. Bitter medicine is read as a hard truth that may still do good. Giving medicine to someone else stands for care and the wish to protect. Forgetting a dose carries the image of neglected self-care. Dream books read the symbol, not a health message — it is about what feels in need of mending.
