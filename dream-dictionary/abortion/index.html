Abortion in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

An abortion in a dream is read symbolically, as a plan or path that was ended before it took full shape. Dream books treat it calmly as a hard choice, relief, regret or all three, never as a judgement on the dreamer. Choosing it yourself is usually linked to a decision to stop something you could not carry, while watching someone else is read as another person’s choice touching your life. Grief in the dream points to feelings about an ending not yet fully mourned. The image belongs to change and choice, not to a verdict.
