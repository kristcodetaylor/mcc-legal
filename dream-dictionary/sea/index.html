Sea in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

The sea stands for the unconscious itself — vast, deep and older than you. A calm sea is read as inner peace, a rough one as a restless mind. Swimming in the sea points to how freely you move among your own feelings. Big waves crashing toward you are linked to emotions or events that feel larger than you. Gazing at the sea from the shore is read as longing, or as a wish for a wider life; sailing across it stands for a journey of change. Dream books ask whether the sea in your dream invited you in or kept you at a distance.
