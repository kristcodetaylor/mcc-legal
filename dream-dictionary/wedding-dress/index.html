Wedding dress in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A wedding dress is traditionally read as hopes about a new role and how you wish to be seen in it. Trying one on, or seeing it stained or torn, is read as feelings about readiness for that change. A beautiful dress that fits perfectly is taken as confidence in a new chapter; one that will not close or feels wrong as doubt about whether the role suits you. Wearing a wedding dress with no wedding in sight is read as longing for celebration or commitment in general. A dress in a colour other than white is read as wanting to do things your own way. The dream reflects how you feel about becoming someone new.
