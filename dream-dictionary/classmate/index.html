Classmate in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A classmate from the past is read as a reminder of who you were then and what you were learning. Dream books link the figure to comparison, old dynamics or an unfinished lesson.

A question for you

Has this come up in your dreams before? What was going on in your life then?
