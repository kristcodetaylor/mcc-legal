Finding a dead body in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Coming upon a body is read as discovering something that has ended — a feeling, a relationship, an old self — and has not yet been dealt with. Dream books see it as a call to acknowledge a loss, not as a forecast. Finding a stranger is linked to an unknown part of yourself, while finding someone familiar points to a change in that relationship. Calling for help is read as wanting support to face a difficult truth. It is read calmly and symbolically. It can help to notice what in your life has ended quietly without a goodbye.

A question for you

Has this come up in your dreams before? What was going on in your life then?
