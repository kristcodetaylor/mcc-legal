Searching in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Searching for something is read as a need that has no name yet. What you look for — and whether you find it — is taken as a picture of that need. Searching for a person points to missing closeness or a quality they stand for, while searching for a lost object is read as fear of losing something of value in waking life. Going through room after room without knowing what you want is a very common version, traditionally read as restlessness or a goal that is still unclear. Finding the thing at last is taken as clarity arriving. The dream asks what feels missing right now.
