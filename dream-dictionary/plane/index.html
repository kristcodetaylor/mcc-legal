Plane in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A plane is read as high ambition and a fast change of place in life. Taking off is traditionally read as a new project lifting off the ground, and landing as bringing it home. A crash is one of the more common travel nightmares and is read as fear of plans failing, not as a sign that they fail. Turbulence points to a bumpy stretch of a journey that has already begun. Missing the flight is taken as worry about a missed opportunity, while flying the plane yourself suggests taking charge of a big change.
