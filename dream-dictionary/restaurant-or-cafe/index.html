Restaurant or café in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A restaurant is read as being served and as social life in public. Waiting for food that does not come traditionally stands for a need others do not see, while a lavish meal is linked to enjoyment and reward. Eating alone in a restaurant points to independence or loneliness, depending on the feeling. Not being able to pay the bill is read as worry about what something costs you. Ordering from a menu you cannot read stands for unclear choices. A café with friends carries the image of ease and belonging among people who know you.
