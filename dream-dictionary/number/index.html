Number in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A number that stands out in a dream is read through what it means to you — an age, a date, a house, a count. Dream books treat it as a pointer to a personal association rather than a code for what lies ahead.

A question for you

Has this come up in your dreams before? What was going on in your life then?
