Sky in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

The sky stands for the mind’s open space — freedom, aspiration and the wide view. Clouds are read as thoughts or worries passing across it; a clear blue sky points to peace and clarity. A dark or stormy sky is linked to gloom or tension in the air around you. A sky changing colour, glowing red or strangely lit, is read as a sense that something unusual is shifting in your life. Flying through the sky stands for rising above limits. It can help to notice whether the sky in your dream felt wide and inviting or low and heavy.
