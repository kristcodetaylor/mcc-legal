Stone in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Stone is read as what is solid and lasting — and as a weight. Tradition asks whether it is a foundation under you or a burden on you. Carrying heavy stones is linked to worries or responsibilities taken on without help, while building with stone points to patience and something meant to endure. A stone thrown at you is read as hurtful words or blame, and a precious or shining stone as hidden worth. A heart of stone is the old image of feelings held tightly. It may help to notice what in your life feels unmovable right now.
