Coffee in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Coffee is the ritual that starts the day and opens a conversation, so dream books read it as alertness, sociability and the wish for a fresh start or a little more energy. Drinking it with someone is linked to a talk that matters, or to closeness that grows over small daily moments. Spilled coffee is read as a hurried beginning or a plan that started off on the wrong foot. Coffee that is cold or bitter points to tiredness and to forcing yourself through. It can help to notice where in your days you are running on willpower rather than rest.
