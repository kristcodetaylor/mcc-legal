Wedding in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A wedding is read as a union — of two people, or of two sides of yourself. Dream books also take it as a new stage of life being entered. Marrying a stranger is a common version, read as embracing a quality you are only beginning to know. Being a guest points to watching change in others, while not being able to reach the ceremony is taken as hesitation about a commitment. A wedding to an ex is usually read as revisiting the past rather than wanting it back. A wedding where something goes wrong — the dress, the rings — points to nerves before a big step.
