Fainting in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Fainting is read as a wish to step out of a moment that feels like too much. Dream books also link it to overwhelm, or to letting others catch you for once. Fainting in public points to fear of losing face, while someone else fainting suggests worry that they cannot cope. Being caught as you fall is read as trust that support is there. It can help to notice what in your days you would rather briefly switch off from.
