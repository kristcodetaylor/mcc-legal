Money in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Money is read as worth, including self-worth, and as energy you can spend. Finding it traditionally stands for a value discovered — a talent or a chance — and losing it for a fear of lack. Counting money points to taking stock, while giving it away is read as generosity or as feeling drained. Coins and notes slipping through your fingers is a common version, taken as worry that effort is not adding up. Dream books treat these images as feelings about value, not as hints about real money or what to do with it.
