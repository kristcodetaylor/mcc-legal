Zombie attack in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Being surrounded by the walking dead is read as feeling drained by routine, or by people and demands that keep coming without thought. Tradition also takes it as fear of going through life on autopilot. Barricading yourself inside is usually read as protecting your energy and boundaries. Being caught or turning into a zombie yourself is linked to fear of losing your individuality, while fighting them off stands for reclaiming control. Escaping with a group points to the comfort of allies under pressure. It can help to ask what in your days feels mechanical or lifeless.
