Being stabbed in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A stabbing is traditionally read as betrayal or a wounding remark, especially from someone close. The old phrase about a knife in the back is how many dream books see it. Being stabbed by a friend or partner is read as fear of being let down, not as proof of it. Being attacked by a stranger is taken as vulnerability or feeling exposed. Stabbing someone else is read as anger or the wish to cut a tie, rather than any real intent. Feeling no pain is read as shock or numbness. These dreams describe hurt feelings, calmly and symbolically, and may ask where trust feels fragile.

A question for you
