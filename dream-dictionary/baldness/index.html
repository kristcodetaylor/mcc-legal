Baldness in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Baldness is read as exposure, vulnerability or a fear of losing strength and appeal. Suddenly finding yourself bald is usually taken as anxiety about how you come across, or about losing something that felt like part of your identity. Seeing another person bald may point to noticing their vulnerability, or to seeing them without their usual mask. Dream books also see a calmer side: letting go of pretence and standing as you are. The image often asks what you feel you would be without your usual armour.
