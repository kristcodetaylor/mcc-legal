Can opener in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A can opener gets into something sealed, so tradition reads it as access — the key to a closed situation or a person who keeps to themselves. Not finding one points to frustration.

A question for you

Has this come up in your dreams before? What was going on in your life then?
