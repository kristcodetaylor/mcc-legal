Brake failure in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Brakes that do not work are one of the classic dreams of losing control. They are read as a situation gathering momentum while you feel unable to slow it down — work, a relationship or a habit. Pressing the pedal again and again is linked to effort that does not seem to change anything. Rolling down a hill points to things moving faster than you can think, while someone else’s car failing points to worry about them. Stopping safely in the end is read as finding control after all. It can help to ask where you need permission to slow down or say no.
