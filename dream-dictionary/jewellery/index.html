Jewellery in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Jewellery is read as value, beauty and the things you treasure about yourself. Dream books link gifts of jewellery to affection and recognition, and lost pieces to fear of losing worth or a relationship. Wearing a great deal of it is usually read as the wish to be noticed or to feel special. Finding jewellery points to discovering a talent or quality, while broken pieces are linked to a promise or bond that feels damaged. Inherited jewellery stands for family ties and what has been passed down. It can help to ask what you value most in yourself right now.
