Spider in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

The spider is read as patience and careful making — the web built thread by thread. It can also stand for feeling caught in something, or for a fear you keep avoiding. A spider spinning its web is linked to creativity and to plans you are weaving slowly. Being bitten is traditionally read as a small betrayal or a sharp word that stung more than expected. A large spider in your home points to a worry that has settled in, and killing one is read as finally dealing with that worry. It can help to ask what in your days feels like a web — something you made, or something holding you.
