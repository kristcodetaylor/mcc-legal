Purse in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A purse stands for what you carry of yourself: resources, identity, private things. Losing it in a dream is read as feeling exposed or scattered; a full one as feeling secure. Searching through a purse and not finding something is usually read as missing a piece of yourself or a skill you need. Someone else opening your purse is linked to invaded privacy, while a heavy, overfilled purse points to worries carried around all day. An empty purse is often tied to tiredness rather than literal money. It can help to notice what you most want to keep close.
