Terrorist attack in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A sudden attack in a public place is read as anxiety about safety and the unpredictability of the world. Dream books treat it as tension absorbed from the news or a feeling that stability can break without warning. Hiding during an attack is read as wanting protection; helping others as care and responsibility under pressure. Being caught up in chaos is taken as feeling overwhelmed by events you cannot control. Such dreams are common after unsettling headlines, and tradition reads them as fear made visible, never as a forecast. It can help to notice which part of your life feels most exposed right now, and what helps you feel steady.
