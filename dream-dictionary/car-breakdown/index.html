Car breakdown in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A car that breaks down on the way is read as a plan or a body of effort that has stalled. It is often linked to tiredness and a need to stop and tend to what carries you.

A question for you

Has this come up in your dreams before? What was going on in your life then?
