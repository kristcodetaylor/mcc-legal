Affair in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

An affair in a dream is read less as a literal event than as divided attention or an unmet need. Whether you are having it or discovering it, tradition takes it as a question about what feels missing. Having an affair yourself is read as longing for a quality the other person represents — excitement, attention, freedom — rather than for them. Discovering a partner’s affair is usually taken as insecurity or fear of being replaced, not as evidence of anything. An affair with a stranger is read as attraction to an unknown side of yourself. Dream books treat these as some of the most common relationship dreams, and as a mirror rather than a verdict.
