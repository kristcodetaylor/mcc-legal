Necklace in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A necklace is read as connection and what lies close to the heart and throat. Tradition links it to bonds, to identity — and, if it feels tight, to words held back. Receiving one as a gift is usually read as affection or a commitment being offered. A broken necklace is linked to a relationship or promise that feels strained, while beads scattering across the floor point to things coming apart. Wearing a pendant with special meaning stands for keeping a person or value close. It can help to notice which bonds feel most precious to you now.
