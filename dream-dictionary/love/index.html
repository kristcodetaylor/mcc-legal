Love in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Love is read as the wish to belong and to be accepted. Feeling loved in a dream traditionally points to something in you that is being reconciled. Falling in love with a stranger is linked to a quality you are drawn to or want to grow in yourself, while love for an ex or an old friend points to a memory still asking for a place. Unrequited love is read as longing and feeling unseen. Declaring love stands for courage and openness. Dream books often see such dreams as a warm sign of self-acceptance rather than a message about a specific person.
