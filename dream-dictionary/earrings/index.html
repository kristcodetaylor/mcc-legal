Earrings in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Earrings are read as attention to what you hear and how you present yourself. Tradition links them to self-expression and to listening with care. Losing an earring is a common variant, usually read as missing part of a conversation or feeling slightly incomplete. Receiving earrings as a gift is linked to being noticed or appreciated, while heavy, painful earrings point to something you are tired of hearing. Choosing a new pair stands for a change in how you show yourself to others. It can help to ask what you have been listening to closely lately.
