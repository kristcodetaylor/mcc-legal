Dinosaur in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Dinosaurs stand for the past — old ways, outdated beliefs or big forces from long ago that still loom over the present. Dream books read a living dinosaur as something that should be extinct but is still walking around: an old fear, an old conflict, an old rule. Being chased by one is taken as running from a problem that feels too large and too ancient to fight. A gentle dinosaur, or a skeleton in a museum, is read as looking calmly at history you have already outgrown. Some readings link it to feeling old-fashioned yourself, out of step with a world that has moved on. Ask what in your life is bigger than it needs to be because it has never been retired.

A question for you

Where in your waking life have you met something like this lately?
