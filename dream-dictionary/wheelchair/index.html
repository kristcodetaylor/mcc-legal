Wheelchair in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A wheelchair is read as a need for support or a different way of moving forward. Tradition sees it as accepting help, or as a phase where you feel dependent on others. Being in one yourself is usually taken as feeling slowed down or held back, while pushing someone else points to caring for a person who relies on you. Getting up out of a wheelchair is read as regaining independence or confidence. The dream speaks of support and movement, not of the body.
