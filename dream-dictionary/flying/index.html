Flying in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Flying is read as freedom and rising above limits. An easy flight stands for confidence; struggling to stay up, for doubt about how far you can go. Soaring high points to ambition and a broad perspective; flying low or being unable to take off is linked to obstacles or self-doubt. Flying away from danger is read as escape from pressure. Losing the ability to fly mid-air stands for fear of losing what you have achieved. Lucid flying is a celebrated experience of joy and control; it can help to notice what felt lighter when you were airborne.
