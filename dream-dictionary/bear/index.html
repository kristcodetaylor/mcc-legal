Bear in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A bear is read as raw strength and protective, almost parental force. It also keeps the idea of retreat — the winter sleep, time taken alone to recover. A calm bear is traditionally linked to inner power and to someone strong watching over you. Being chased or attacked by a bear points to an overwhelming situation, or to a person whose temper feels hard to predict. A mother bear with cubs stands for fierce protectiveness, and a sleeping bear for strength you are holding in reserve. It can help to notice whether you need more rest or more courage right now.
