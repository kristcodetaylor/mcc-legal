Miscarriage in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A dream of miscarriage is traditionally read as a plan, hope or project that did not come to term, and the grief that belongs to it. It can surface after a disappointment, or when something you invested in quietly stopped growing. Dreaming of someone else’s miscarriage may reflect worry for them, or a shared loss. For people who are pregnant or hoping to be, such dreams are a common expression of fear and tenderness. It is a symbol of loss and anxiety, not a forecast about any pregnancy.
