Being unprepared in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Being unprepared is one of the most common anxiety dreams, read as fear of being tested before you feel ready. It reflects pressure and self-doubt, not a real failure ahead. Arriving at an exam you never studied for is linked to worry about being judged, while standing on stage without knowing your lines points to fear of exposure. Showing up without the right clothes or documents is read as a sense of not belonging in a new role. Often this dream comes to people who prepare very carefully. It can help to notice which upcoming situation makes you feel you must be perfect.
