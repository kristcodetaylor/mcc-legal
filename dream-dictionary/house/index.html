House in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

The house is the dream book’s picture of the self. Its rooms are read as parts of your life: the attic for thoughts and memory, the cellar for what is kept below, the kitchen for care and nourishment. A house in good repair is taken as feeling settled, while a crumbling or flooded one points to a part of life that needs attention. Discovering new rooms is one of the best-loved variants, read as room to grow that had gone unnoticed. Returning to a childhood house is traditionally read as old patterns or memories coming back into view. It can help to ask which room of your life you spend the least time in.
