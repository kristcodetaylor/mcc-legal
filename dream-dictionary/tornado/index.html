Tornado in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A twister stands for sudden, disruptive change and emotions whirling out of control. Dream books read watching one approach as anxiety about something unpredictable on its way, especially a situation that feels too fast to steer. Hiding from it in a cellar or shelter is read as the wish to protect yourself until things calm down. Being caught up by the wind stands for feeling swept along by other people’s moods or decisions. Seeing it pass at a distance is read as tension that brushed close but left you standing. It can help to notice where life has been spinning lately.

A question for you

Has this come up in your dreams before? What was going on in your life then?
