Hotel in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A hotel is read as a temporary stage — a place you pass through, not one you stay in. Tradition links it to transition and to a self that is between two homes. A luxurious hotel is linked to rest and the wish to be looked after, while a shabby one points to a phase that feels uncomfortable. Getting lost in endless hotel corridors or forgetting your room number is a common dream about losing your place during change. Checking out is read as being ready to move on. The dream may ask what in your life feels temporary right now.
