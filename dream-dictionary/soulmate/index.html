Soulmate in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Meeting a soulmate in a dream is traditionally read as a longing to be fully understood. Dream books also see the figure as a part of yourself you are ready to recognise and welcome. A soulmate with no clear face is read as an ideal rather than a person — the qualities you most want close. Feeling deep recognition with a stranger is taken as meeting a new side of your own character. Losing your soulmate or searching for them through a crowd is read as loneliness, or as fear of settling for less than you need. The dream asks what complete closeness would feel like, and where you already find pieces of it.
