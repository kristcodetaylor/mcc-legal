666 in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

In the Book of Revelation 666 is the number of the beast, and popular culture made it a sign of menace, while in Chinese the same digits sound like “smooth”. Dream books read it as fear of temptation or of a shadow side — a feeling to look at, not a verdict on anyone.
