Sandstorm in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A sandstorm blinds and disorients, so it stands for confusion and stress that make it hard to see the way. Dream books read sheltering from it as waiting for clarity to return.

A question for you

Where in your waking life have you met something like this lately?
