Storm in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A storm is traditionally read as inner conflict or a feeling that has built up and breaks. Lightning adds sudden insight — something seen all at once. Watching a storm from a safe place is linked to observing turmoil around you without being part of it. Being caught in one points to stress, arguments or change that feels out of your hands. A storm that passes and leaves calm skies is read as relief after a difficult time. Thunder stands for news or anger that cannot be ignored; it can help to notice which tension in your days feels close to breaking.
