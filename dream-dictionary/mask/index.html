Mask in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A mask is read as the face shown in place of the real one. Dream books ask who is hiding — someone else, or you. Wearing a mask is linked to playing a role at work, in a family or among friends, while being unable to take it off points to feeling stuck in that role. Someone in a mask is read as a person whose intentions feel unclear. A festive mask stands for play and permission to be someone else for a while. Removing a mask carries the image of honesty and relief, the moment when what was hidden can be seen.
