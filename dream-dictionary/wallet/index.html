Wallet in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A wallet is read as self-worth and identity as much as money. Dream books link a full wallet to confidence in your resources and an empty one to feeling drained or undervalued. Finding a wallet is usually read as discovering a resource or quality you did not know you had, while returning it to its owner points to honesty and integrity. A stolen wallet is linked to feeling taken advantage of. Handing your wallet over stands for generosity, or for giving away too much of yourself. It can help to ask what you feel you are carrying and how you value it.
