Merkaba in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

In Jewish mystical tradition the Merkabah is the divine chariot of the prophet’s vision; modern spiritual language uses the name for a star-shaped body of light. In a dream it is read as ascent, protection and the wish to rise above the everyday.
