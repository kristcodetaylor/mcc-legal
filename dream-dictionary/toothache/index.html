Toothache in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A nagging toothache is read as a problem you keep putting off — small at first, but harder to ignore the longer it is left. Dream books link it to a word unsaid or a conflict that keeps throbbing in the background. A sudden, sharp toothache points to a fresh hurt, often in a close relationship. Seeing someone else in pain from a tooth suggests worry about their troubles. The dream speaks of tension and neglect, not of the teeth.
