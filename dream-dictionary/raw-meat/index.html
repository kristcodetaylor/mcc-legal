Raw meat in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Uncooked meat stands for raw instinct and things not yet processed. Dream tradition reads it as an unrefined emotion or a situation still too fresh to handle calmly. Handling raw meat is usually read as dealing directly with something uncomfortable. Eating it is linked to impulsive desires or impatience, while cooking it points to working something through until it is ready. Spoiled raw meat stands for a problem left too long. It can help to ask what feeling in you is still raw.
