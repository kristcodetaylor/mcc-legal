Lizard in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

The lizard basks in the sun and slips away when threatened. Dream books read it as instinct, quick survival skills and the ability to regenerate — the tail that grows back after it is lost. A lizard warming itself on a rock is read as a need for rest and slowness; one darting away as an opportunity or a feeling that escapes before you can catch it. A lizard losing its tail is taken as giving something up in order to get free. Some traditions read a lizard inside the house as small, cold-blooded thoughts creeping in. The dream often asks how quickly you adapt, and what you would be willing to leave behind to move on.
