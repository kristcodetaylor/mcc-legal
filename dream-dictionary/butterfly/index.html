Butterfly in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

The butterfly is the classic symbol of transformation — a life that changes form. It is also read as lightness, and as something beautiful that does not stay long. A butterfly landing on you is traditionally linked to a gentle change or a welcome visitor. Many butterflies point to joy and freedom, while a caterpillar or cocoon speaks of change still in progress. A dead or trapped butterfly is read as a fragile hope that needs protecting. In several folk traditions a butterfly is also seen as the soul of someone remembered; it can help to ask what in your days is quietly changing shape.
