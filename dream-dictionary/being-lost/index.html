Being lost in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Being lost is read as uncertainty about direction — not knowing which way is yours. Tradition takes it as a prompt to stop and get your bearings, not as a failure. Lost in a familiar town, the dream is usually read as feeling out of place in your own life; lost in an unknown city, as a new phase whose rules are not clear yet. Searching for the way home is one of the most common versions and points to longing for safety and belonging. Asking for directions is a hopeful detail, read as being ready to accept help.
