Trial in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A trial is read as guilt, accountability and fear of judgement. Tradition treats it as an inner court where you put your own choices on the stand. Being on trial is read as feeling scrutinised at work, at home or by your own conscience. Being found innocent is taken as relief and self-acceptance; being found guilty as harshness towards yourself rather than as a forecast. Serving as a witness is read as being asked to speak up about something you have seen. Sitting on a jury is read as weighing someone else’s actions. This is a dream about evaluation, and it may ask what you are judging yourself for.
