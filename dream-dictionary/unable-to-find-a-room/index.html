Unable to find a room in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Searching hallways for a room you cannot find is read as looking for your place — a role, a home or a belonging that keeps slipping away. Tradition links it to disorientation during change. Numbers that skip or doors that lead to the wrong rooms are read as plans that do not add up the way you expected. Being late for something in that room points to pressure, while wandering calmly suggests curiosity about who you are becoming. Finding a room you never knew existed is read as discovering a new capacity. It can help to notice where in your life you feel slightly lost in the corridors.
