Planet in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A planet stands for a separate world: a project, a relationship or a part of yourself with its own rules. Visiting an unknown one is read as exploring unfamiliar inner territory. Seeing a giant planet hanging in the sky is usually read as something that looms large in your thoughts. Standing on a barren planet is linked to loneliness or distance from others, while a colourful, inhabited one points to curiosity and new possibilities. Planets lining up or colliding are read as parts of life coming into alignment or conflict. It can help to notice which part of your life feels like a world of its own.
