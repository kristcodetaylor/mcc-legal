Depression in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Depression in a dream is read as heaviness given a shape — grey light, slow movement, weight on the chest. Tradition takes it as an inner state asking to be noticed, not a verdict. Watching someone else in that state points to worry for them, or to seeing your own mood in another face. Light breaking through, or colour returning, is read as hope and a turn in feeling. If that heaviness stays with you in waking life too, sharing it with someone you trust can lighten it.
