Becoming invisible in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Becoming invisible carries two readings: the pain of being overlooked, and the relief of being free from others’ gaze. Which one fits depends on whether the dream felt lonely or liberating. Calling out to people who do not see or hear you is linked to feeling unheard in a group or a relationship. Choosing to become invisible on purpose is read as a wish to escape attention, pressure or judgement. Slipping unseen into a place you are not allowed is read as curiosity about a side of life closed to you. It can help to notice where you would like to be seen more — or less.
