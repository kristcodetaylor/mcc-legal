Face in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

The face is read as identity and the self you show to the world. A face you cannot make out is traditionally someone, or something in you, not yet recognised. Seeing your own face changed — older, younger, unfamiliar — is linked to a shift in how you see yourself. A blank or faceless figure points to anonymity or to a role that hides the person. A beautiful face is read as acceptance, a frightening one as a fear given features. Washing your face carries the old image of wanting a clean slate in how others see you.
