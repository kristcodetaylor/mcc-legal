Bell in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A bell is read as a call for attention — news, a warning or a moment to mark. Tradition takes it as something asking to be heard. Church bells are linked to celebration, gathering and turning points such as weddings or farewells, while a doorbell points to someone or something waiting to come in. A bell ringing in the distance is read as a memory or a call from the past. A bell that will not ring stands for a message that cannot get through. The dream may ask what you have been ignoring that keeps knocking quietly.

A question for you
