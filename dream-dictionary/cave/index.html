Cave in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A cave is read as a way inward — the hidden room of the self, where old memories are kept. Going in is traditionally a search for something buried. Being lost or trapped in a cave is linked to fear of the unknown or to feeling closed in by a situation. Finding light or treasure inside points to insight that comes from looking deeply; a cave as shelter is read as a need for retreat and rest. Basements, cellars and tunnels carry a similar meaning of what lies beneath the surface. It can help to ask what you might be ready to look at more closely.
