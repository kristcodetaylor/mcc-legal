Crown and royalty in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A crown is read as authority, success and responsibility. A king or queen traditionally stands for the ruling part of you — or a parent seen large. Wearing a crown is linked to recognition and to stepping into your own power, while a heavy crown points to the weight that comes with it. Losing a crown is read as fear of losing status or control. A broken or false crown stands for authority that feels hollow. Meeting royalty is a common dream about measuring yourself against those above you, or about wanting to be treated with honour.
