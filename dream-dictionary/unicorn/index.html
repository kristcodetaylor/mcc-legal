Unicorn in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

The unicorn stands for purity, wonder and the rare — an ideal that feels too good to be real, or innocence worth protecting. Old legends say it can be approached only by the gentle, so dream books link it to trust and to a softness you may be guarding. Riding a unicorn is read as moving towards a dream or a high hope; touching its horn as contact with something healing. A wounded or captured unicorn is taken as an ideal under pressure, or innocence that feels threatened. Chasing one that always slips away is read as longing for perfection. The dream may ask what you still believe in, and how you keep it safe.
