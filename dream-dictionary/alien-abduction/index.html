Alien abduction in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Being taken by aliens is read as feeling powerless before forces you cannot understand or control. Dream tradition treats it as an image of overwhelm or intrusion, not a literal event. Being lifted into a craft is usually read as being pulled away from your normal life by work, duties or other people’s plans. Being examined or watched is linked to feeling scrutinised or exposed, while escaping or being returned home stands for regaining your footing. These dreams often come in times of stress or broken sleep. It can help to ask where your choices feel taken out of your hands.
