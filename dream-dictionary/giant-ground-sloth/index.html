Giant ground sloth in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Huge and slow, the giant ground sloth is read as heavy, unhurried strength. Dream tradition links it to something old and massive in your life that moves at its own pace.

A question for you

Where in your waking life have you met something like this lately?
