Palace in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A palace stands for grandeur, power and the richness of the inner self. Dream books read wandering its halls as discovering your own worth, or as feeling small in a world of high expectations. Being welcomed in points to recognition and confidence, while being turned away suggests feeling excluded from a world you admire. Living in a palace is read as a wish for comfort and status. It can help to ask where you are seeking acknowledgement right now.
