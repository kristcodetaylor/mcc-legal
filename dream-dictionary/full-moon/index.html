Full moon in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

The full moon is traditionally read as completion, ripeness and feelings at their fullest. Folklore also ties it to heightened emotion, restless nights and hidden things coming to light. A huge, glowing moon low on the horizon is usually read as a strong emotion or intuition demanding attention. Moonlight on water is linked to calm reflection and the inner life, while a full moon half hidden behind clouds stands for feelings partly obscured. Many cultures treated the full moon as a time of culmination before the next cycle begins. It can help to ask what in your life has reached its peak.
