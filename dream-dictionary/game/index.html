Game in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A game is read as rivalry, rules and risk taken lightly. Winning or losing in a dream traditionally speaks of how you measure yourself against others. Playing with children is linked to lightness and to an old, playful part of you, while cheating or being cheated points to a sense that the rules are unfair. A game that never ends is read as a situation that keeps repeating. Not knowing the rules stands for feeling unprepared in a new setting. The dream may ask whether something in your waking life is being treated as a game.
