Bridge in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

A bridge is the symbol of transition and connection — the way from one shore of life to another. A broken or shaky bridge is read as doubt about a step ahead; crossing a bridge with ease points to a change you are ready for. Stopping halfway is linked to indecision, or to standing between two stages of life. A bridge collapsing is read as fear that a link with someone or a plan will not hold. Building a bridge stands for reconciliation and reaching out. Dream books ask what lies on the other side of the bridge you were crossing.
