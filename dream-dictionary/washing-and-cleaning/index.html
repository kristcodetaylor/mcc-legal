Washing and cleaning in a dream: meaning

What dream books say

Washing is read as clearing away — guilt, a bad day, an old state. Tradition takes it as the wish for a fresh start. Washing your hands is linked to wanting to be free of responsibility for something, while cleaning a house points to putting your life in order. Dirt that will not come off is read as a feeling or memory that lingers. Washing someone else stands for care and devotion. Doing laundry carries the old image of sorting private matters. It can help to notice what you most wanted clean in the dream.
