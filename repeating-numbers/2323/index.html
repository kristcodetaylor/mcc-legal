23:23 and 2323: meaning in numerology

23:23 is the last double time before midnight, and it adds up to 10, then 1.

The numerology of 23:23

What tradition reads it as

Each 23 reduces to 5, the number of change and freedom. The whole, 2+3+2+3 = 10, reduces to 1, a beginning. So the last double time of the day is read as change that leads to a new start, the day closing on an opening. Twenty-three is a prime number, divisible only by 1 and itself, and it has long had a following of its own among people who notice it everywhere, which is part of why this time is among the most often reported.
