What tradition reads it as

Twenty-two is the master builder of numerology, large plans made real, joining the vision of 11 with the structure of 4. In 22:22 it appears twice and is not reduced. The whole, 2+2+2+2, is 8, the number of mastery and material power. Read together, 22:22 is the most practical of the master-number times: ambition with the means to carry it out. It also reads the same backwards, and it comes late in the evening, when the day’s work is usually done.
