11:11 and 1111: meaning in numerology

11:11 is the most talked-about repeating time of all. It is two master numbers side by side, and it adds up to 4.

What tradition reads it as

Numerology sees two layers in 11:11. Each half is 11, the master number of intuition and vision, which is not reduced further. The whole, 1+1+1+1, is 4, the number of structure and foundations. Read together: insight that wants a solid form, an idea that asks to be built. It is also a mirror, 1111 read either way, and on a 12-hour clock it comes twice a day. The date 11 November is Armistice Day in many countries and Singles’ Day in China, both chosen for the row of ones.

Many people call numbers like this angel numbers. We read them through numerology: the digits, the sum and what tradition says about each number, with no forecasts and no hidden messages.
