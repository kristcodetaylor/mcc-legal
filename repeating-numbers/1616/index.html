What tradition reads it as

Sixteen is read as the karmic-debt number of the ego released and something rebuilt truer. The whole, 1+6+1+6 = 14, is itself a karmic-debt number, moderation, and reduces to 5, change. That makes 16:16 one of the few times carrying two karmic numbers at once, and tradition reads it as a change that asks you to let go of how things looked before rebuilding them more honestly. Sixteen is also the number of the Tower in the Major Arcana, the card of sudden change.
