10:10 and 1010: meaning in numerology

10:10 is the time on almost every watch in an advertisement, and it adds up to 2.

The numerology of 10:10

Sum: 1+0+1+0 = 2 · Core number 2

  • 10 repeats

What tradition reads it as

Each half is 10, a one and a zero: a beginning next to an empty circle, which numerology reads as a fresh start in a new cycle. Ten itself reduces to 1. The whole, 1+0+1+0, is 2, the number of partnership, so 10:10 is read as a new start shared with someone, or two new starts side by side. Watchmakers set their hands to 10:10 in photos because the hands frame the logo and look like a smile, which is one reason this time feels familiar before you notice it on your own clock.
