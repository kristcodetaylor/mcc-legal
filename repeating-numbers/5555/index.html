What tradition reads it as

Five is the number of change and freedom, and four in a row make it the whole picture: things in motion. The sum, 20, reduces to 2, partnership and balance. Numerology reads 5555 as a lot of change that is best handled together with someone, rather than alone: a busy, shifting time in which cooperation keeps things steady. It is the number of the restless traveller with someone sitting beside them.
