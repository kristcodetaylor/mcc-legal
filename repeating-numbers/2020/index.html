20:20 and 2020: meaning in numerology

20:20 is two and zero twice, and it adds up to 4. In English it is also the phrase for perfect eyesight.

What tradition reads it as

Each half is 20, a two next to an empty circle: partnership with room to grow, reducing to 2. The whole, 2+0+2+0, is 4, structure and foundations. Tradition reads 20:20 as a relationship or partnership given a solid shape, something agreed and built on. "20/20 vision" means seeing clearly at twenty feet, and many people link the time to seeing a situation for what it is. Twenty is also Judgement in the Major Arcana, a card of awakening.
