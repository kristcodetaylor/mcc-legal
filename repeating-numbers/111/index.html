111 meaning in numerology

111 is three ones in a row, and it adds up to 3. On any 12-hour clock it shows up twice a day, at 1:11.

What tradition reads it as

In numerology 1 is the number of beginnings and independence, and three of them in a row are read as that quality said three times: a start, a first step, an idea taken up by one person. The sum, 3, is the number of expression, so tradition reads 111 as a beginning that wants to be said out loud or made visible rather than kept in your head. Of all the triples it is the one people mention most, perhaps because a run of ones is so easy to spot.
