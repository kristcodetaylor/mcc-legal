18:18 and 1818: meaning in numerology

18:18 is 18 twice, and it adds up to 18, then 9: completion, and completion again.

What tradition reads it as

Each 18 reduces to 9, the number of completion, compassion and letting go, and so does the whole, 1+8+1+8 = 18, 1+8 = 9. Every part of 18:18 lands on the same digit, which numerology reads as an ending seen from every side: a cycle that is ready to close. In Jewish tradition 18 is the numerical value of "chai", life, and gifts are often given in multiples of 18, a reminder that an ending in numerology is also what makes room for what follows.
