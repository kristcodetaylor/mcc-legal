What tradition reads it as

Fourteen is read in numerology as the karmic-debt number of moderation: freedom used wisely, change without excess. Each 14 reduces to 5, the number of change and movement, and the whole, 1+4+1+4 = 10, reduces to 1, a beginning. Read together, 14:14 is about a start that comes from changing at the right pace, neither clinging to the old nor rushing past it. It only appears on a 24-hour clock, in the middle of the afternoon.
