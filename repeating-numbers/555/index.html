555 meaning in numerology

555 is three fives, and it adds up to 15, then 6. It is the triple of change.

What tradition reads it as

Five is the number of change, freedom and movement, and it resists standing still. Three fives turn that up: a period that will not stay the same. The sum, 15, reduces to 6, the number of care, home and responsibility, so tradition reads 555 as change that reaches the home and the people in it: a move, a new routine, a shift in who looks after whom. In Thai, 5 is pronounced "ha", so 555 is written online for laughter.
