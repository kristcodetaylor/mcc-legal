1234 meaning in numerology

1234 counts up one step at a time, and it adds up to 10, then 1.

What tradition reads it as

A sequence is read in numerology as momentum, each step leading into the next. 1234 runs through the first four numbers: a beginning (1), a partner (2), expression (3) and a foundation (4). The sum, 10, reduces to 1, so the run ends where it started, at a beginning, one level up. Tradition reads it as progress made in order, step by step. It is also one of the most common PIN codes in the world, which is why it is worth changing yours if it is 1234.
