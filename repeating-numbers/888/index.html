888 meaning in numerology

888 is three eights, and it adds up to 24, then 6. In Chinese it is one of the most wanted numbers there is.

The numerology of 888

Sum: 8+8+8 = 24 → 2+4 = 6 · Core number 6

  • Mirror: reads the same both ways

What tradition reads it as

Eight is the number of mastery: ambition, authority and the balance between material and inner power. Three eights emphasise it. The sum, 24, reduces to 6, care and responsibility, so numerology reads 888 as ambition put to work for the people around you rather than for itself. In Chinese, 8 sounds like the word for prosperity, so 888 is prized in phone numbers, prices and addresses; the Beijing Olympics opened on 8 August 2008 at 8 p.m.
