What tradition reads it as

Each 21 reduces to 3, expression, and the whole, 2+1+2+1, is 6, care, home and responsibility. Two and one side by side are read as the partner and the self, the mirror image of 12:12. Tradition reads 21:21 as warmth expressed at home, the evening time for the people you live with. Twenty-one is the number of the World, the last card of the Major Arcana, the card of a journey completed, and in many countries 21 is the age of full adulthood.
