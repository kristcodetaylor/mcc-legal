What tradition reads it as

Thirteen is one of the four karmic-debt numbers in numerology, read as the lesson of focus and honest work over shortcuts. Here it appears twice. The whole, 1+3+1+3, is 8, the number of mastery and authority. Tradition reads that pairing straightforwardly: mastery that is earned the slow way. Thirteen has a long history as an unlucky number in the West, and many hotels have no thirteenth floor; numerology does not treat it as a misfortune, but as a lesson about effort.
