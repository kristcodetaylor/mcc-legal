15:15 and 1515: meaning in numerology

15:15 is a quarter past three in the afternoon on a 24-hour clock. It adds up to 12, then 3.

The numerology of 15:15

Sum: 1+5+1+5 = 12 → 1+2 = 3 · Core number 3

  • 15 repeats

What tradition reads it as

Each half is 15, a one and a five: a beginning and a change, which reduces to 6, care and home. The whole, 1+5+1+5 = 12, reduces to 3, the number of expression. Tradition reads 15:15 as change in the home that wants to be talked about, rather than handled in silence. Fifteen is the number of the Devil, card XV of the Major Arcana, a card about attachments and habits, which some readers link to this time as a prompt to look at what holds you in place.

Many people call numbers like this angel numbers. We read them through numerology: the digits, the sum and what tradition says about each number, with no forecasts and no hidden messages.
