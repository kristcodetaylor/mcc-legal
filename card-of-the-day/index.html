Card of the Day: draw one card

Draw one card for today from the full deck of 78. You see its name, whether it came up reversed, its keywords, its theme and a question to sit with, just as the app shows it.

What you see

  • The card’s name: one of the 22 cards from The Fool (0) to The World (21), or one of the 56 cards of the four suits, Wands, Cups, Swords and Pentacles.
  • Whether it came up upright or reversed. You can switch reversed cards off.
  • Three keywords, which change when the card is reversed.
  • The card’s theme and a question to sit with.

How the card is drawn

All 78 cards are shuffled and one is taken. The card is chosen before any text is shown, and the text never changes it: you always see the words written for that card. With reversed cards allowed, a coin flip decides which way up it lands.

Private by design

In the app: no ads, no analytics, no trackers, no account. Your birth details, chart and journal are stored only on your device, and you can export or delete them in the app. The card on this page is drawn in your browser, and nothing is sent or stored.

Questions

Is the Card of the Day random?

Yes. The whole deck is shuffled every time you draw, here and in the app. Nothing about you goes into the draw.

What does a reversed card mean?

It is a card that came up upside down, and it shows its reversed keywords instead of the upright ones. Switch off Allow reversed cards and every card comes up upright.

Does the card say what will happen today?

No. It gives keywords, a theme and a question to think about: possible themes, not a prediction. Your choices and circumstances shape your day.

Which languages are the cards in?

In all 13 languages of the app. The card names, keywords and questions are written in each of them.
