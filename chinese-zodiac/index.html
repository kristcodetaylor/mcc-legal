Chinese horoscope

  • Your year animal and element, counted from Chinese New Year (dates for 1901–2099).
  • Your month animal, from the position of the Sun, and your hour animal when you know your birth time.
  • Your circle: the two allies, the secret friend and the clashing sign.
  • What this year, 2026, the Year of the Fire Horse, is to your sign.

Japanese horoscope

  • Your eto, the animal of the calendar year (Japan counts it from 1 January, so it can differ from the Chinese one).
  • Your Nine Star Ki star and its element, counted from Risshun, around 4 February.
  • The stars that suit yours, and this year’s star.

Vedic horoscope

  • Your Moon sign in the sidereal zodiac (Lahiri ayanamsha), often one sign earlier than in Western astrology.
  • Your nakshatra, its pada and its lord.
  • With an exact birth time and place: your lagna (rising sign), the nine grahas in their houses and your Vimshottari dasha periods.

Questions

Why is my Chinese zodiac animal not the one I expected?

The Chinese year starts at Chinese New Year, between 21 January and 20 February. If you were born in January or early February, you may belong to the previous year’s animal. The app shows the exact dates of your Chinese year.

What is my Vedic Moon sign?

The sign the Moon was in when you were born, measured against the stars (the sidereal zodiac) rather than the seasons. The two zodiacs are now about 24 degrees apart, so your Vedic Moon sign is often the one before your Western Moon sign.

What is Nine Star Ki?

A Japanese system that gives each year one of nine stars, each with an element. Your star comes from your birth year, counted from Risshun, around 4 February.
