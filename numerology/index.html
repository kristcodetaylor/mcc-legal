How the life path number is reached

The day, the month and the year of your birth are each reduced by adding their digits, then the three results are added and reduced again to a single digit. 11, 22 and 33 are master numbers and are not reduced.

Personal year and personal day

Your personal year uses your birth day and month with the current calendar year, so it changes every January. Your personal day changes every day and is on the Today card.

Questions

How do I calculate my life path number?

Reduce the day, the month and the year of your birth, add them and reduce the total. For 14 March 1991: 1+4 = 5, 0+3 = 3, 1+9+9+1 = 20 → 2, and 5+3+2 = 10 → 1. Life path 1.

What is a personal year?

A number from your birth day and month with the current calendar year, reduced the same way. It runs from January to December.

Are master numbers reduced?

No. 11, 22 and 33 are kept as they are.
