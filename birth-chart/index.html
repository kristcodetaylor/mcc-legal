Questions

How do I find my rising sign?

Enter your birth date, exact birth time and birth place. The rising sign is the sign coming up on the eastern horizon at that moment, so it needs the time and the place, not only the date.

What is the difference between a sun sign and a birth chart?

Your sun sign is one placement: where the Sun was. A birth chart places every planet, the Moon, the ascendant and the twelve houses, and shows the aspects between them.
