My Cosmic Code

Synastry calculator: compatibility from two whole birth charts

Sun-sign compatibility compares two signs. My Cosmic Code compares two whole birth charts, every planet of one person against every planet of the other, and shows where they meet.

What you compare

  • Five areas of life: love, intimacy, friendship, work and wellbeing.
  • Aspects between the two charts (synastry).
  • A composite chart.
  • A house overlay: where one person’s planets fall in the other’s chart.

No score, no percentage

The app does not reduce two people to a number. You see how two charts actually meet and decide for yourself what fits.

Private by design

No ads, no analytics, no trackers, no account. Your birth details, chart and journal are stored only on your device, and you can export or delete them in the app.

Questions

What is synastry?

Comparing two birth charts by the aspects between one person’s planets and the other’s.

Do I need both birth times?

The signs and the aspects between the planets work from the birth dates. The rising sign and the house overlay need an exact birth time and place for both people.

Is it free?

The app is free to download, and in the current version every feature is open. No ads.
