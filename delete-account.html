Account & Data Deletion — My Cosmic Code

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This page exists because Google Play requires every app that offers an account to publish a deletion route reachable without installing the app and without signing in. It applies to My Cosmic Code (com.mycosmiccode.app), published by Krist Code Taylor, a sole proprietorship (obrt) established in Croatia.

It is short for an honest reason: there is nothing for us to delete. The app has no account server and no cloud sync, so no copy of your account or your data has ever reached us. Deletion is therefore something you perform on your own device, immediately, without asking anyone's permission and without any identity check — because the data is already, and only, in your hands.

What "account" means here

Registering in My Cosmic Code does not create a real, cross-device account. The email and password you enter are stored on your phone (mcc.account.v1) and checked only against that local copy. There is no server to hold them, no password-reset email, and no record of your registration anywhere but your own device. See the [Privacy Notice](privacy.html) for the full description, including its security limits.

How to delete your account and data

Option 1 — delete everything, in one step. Me screen → Your data → Delete everything. It removes your profile, journal, partner data, saved Deck readings, saved Future View views, saved Cosmic Reports, recent searches and your local account together, immediately and without an undo. Deleting the local account signs you out.

Option 2 — delete one thing at a time. Each kind of data also keeps its own control, so you can remove one without removing the rest:

Option 3 — uninstall the app. Uninstalling My Cosmic Code removes all of the app's private on-device storage in a single step: account, profile, journal, partner data, saved readings and recent searches together. Nothing is left behind on your phone, and nothing survives elsewhere, because nothing was ever stored elsewhere.

What is kept, and for how long

Nothing is kept by us, at any point — before or after deletion. There is no server-side copy, no backup, no archive and no automatic expiry. Your data lives in the app's private storage until you remove it in-app or uninstall the app, and deletion on your device is the end of it.

The one exception worth naming, because it is not ours to delete: if you used voice dictation, your phone's own operating-system speech-recognition service processed that audio. Since 5 August 2026 the app forces that recognition to run on-device, so the audio does not leave your phone — but any data your operating system may retain is governed by your device manufacturer's and Google's policies, not by this app. It contains no account of yours and is not connected to your My Cosmic Code data.

If you cannot use the in-app controls

Because there is no account server, there is no deletion request for us to process, and no support queue that can reach your device. If you have lost access to the phone the app was installed on, the data is confined to that device: it is not recoverable by us and it is not exposed to us either.
