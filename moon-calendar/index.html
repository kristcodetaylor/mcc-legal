Moon calendar: phases and the Moon’s sign, day by day

The Moon goes through its phases in about 29.5 days and changes zodiac sign every two to three days. My Cosmic Code shows both, tonight’s phase and the sign the Moon is in right now, next to the Moon in your own birth chart.

Moon phase and Moon sign by date

Pick a date to see the Moon’s phase, how much of it is lit and which sign it is in, plus the seven days after it. Add a city to get the days and the times in local time.

Questions

How often does the Moon change sign?

Every two to three days. It passes through all twelve signs in a little over 27 days.

What is my Moon sign?

The sign the Moon was in when you were born. The Moon moves fast, so the app uses your birth date, time and place to find it.

Is it free?

The app is free to download, and in the current version every feature is open. No ads.
